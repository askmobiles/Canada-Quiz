Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Canada Quiz & Family Fun Games ("we", "our", or "the site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you use canada-quiz.com and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details to use the quizzes and games. You can use the whole site without signing up.

Like most websites, our hosting and advertising partners may automatically collect standard technical information such as your browser type, device type, general location (country/region), and pages visited. This is used to keep the site working and to show relevant ads.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Privacy & Terms.

Children's privacy

Our site is family-friendly and enjoyed by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask children for names, emails, photos, or any personal details. If you are a parent and have any concern, please contact us.

Links to other websites

Our site may link to other websites (for example, official Government of Canada pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

Your choices

You can turn off cookies in your web browser settings at any time. Some parts of ads may not work as well if cookies are disabled, but the quizzes and games will still work.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: info@canada-quiz.com.
