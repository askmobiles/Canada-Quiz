Which Canadian National Park Are You?

Seven questions about landscapes, crowds and how far you are willing to walk.

📖 How to play

Tap the choice that feels most like you. There are seven questions, no right answers and nothing to study. At the end you get one result, plus the one that came second, and a button to run the whole thing again.

About this quiz

Seven questions about views, crowds, weather and how far you are willing to walk, and one Canadian national park at the end. There is nothing to revise and nothing to get wrong.

The seven parks were picked to be as different from each other as the country allows: the Rockies, the Pacific rainforest, a Newfoundland fjord, the highest tides in the world, a forest island on the prairie, and a granite valley in the Arctic.

It takes about a minute, and it doubles as a gentle introduction to how much of Canada is set aside as parkland.

What's inside

  • Seven questions, four choices each, with a progress bar.
  • Seven results: Banff, Jasper, Gros Morne, Pacific Rim, Fundy, Riding Mountain and Auyuittuq.
  • Each result describes the character of the place rather than listing its facilities.
  • A Try again button restarts the quiz instantly.
  • Free, no signup, and it works on a phone.

Good to know

  • Banff, founded in 1885, was Canada's first national park and the third in the world.
  • Jasper is the largest national park in the Rocky Mountains and is also a dark-sky preserve.
  • Gros Morne is a UNESCO World Heritage Site because its Tablelands expose rock from the Earth's mantle at the surface.
  • The Bay of Fundy, beside Fundy National Park, has the highest tides recorded anywhere on Earth.
  • Auyuittuq is on Baffin Island in Nunavut, and its name comes from Inuktitut for the land that never melts.

Common questions

Are these the only national parks in Canada?

No, there are dozens. These seven were chosen because they are as unlike each other as possible, which makes for a better quiz.

How long does it take?

About a minute, seven questions.

Is this a travel guide?

No. It is a personality quiz. Check the official park pages for fees, seasons, permits and safety before planning a visit.

Can I get a different park?

Yes. Change two answers and the totals move.
