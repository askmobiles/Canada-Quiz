🗳️ Ontario's 2026 municipal election results

Who won on October 26, 2026 in 42 of Ontario's biggest cities and towns: the mayor, how many councillors each council has, and who was elected to each seat. Tap a city to see its whole council.

Unofficial results · last checked 27 October 2026, noon Eastern

These are unofficial results. Cities count the votes on election night and declare the official results a few days later. When a race was very close, or we could not yet confirm a name with the city's own results page or two news reports, this page says Not yet confirmed. We would rather wait than guess, and we fill those in as the cities publish.

Jump to: At a glance · All 42 mayors · City by city · Common questions

At a glance

  • 16 mayors were re-elected and 24 cities chose a new mayor.
  • 2 mayors were acclaimed: no one ran against them, so they won without a vote.
  • In 14 cities the mayor who was running again lost: Mississauga, Hamilton, Windsor, Oakville, Cambridge, Kingston, Milton, Waterloo, Chatham-Kent, Clarington, Niagara Falls, Halton Hills, Belleville, Cornwall.
  • Gord Krantz, mayor of Milton since 1980, lost to George Minakakis.
  • Closest mayor's race: Welland, where Pat Chiocchio won by 55 votes.
  • Too close to call so far, so a recount is possible: Waterloo, regional council seat (3 votes apart); Kitchener, Ward 5 (7 votes apart); Clarington, Ward 1 (8 votes apart); Kitchener, Ward 10 (12 votes apart); Oshawa, regional council seat (18 votes apart).
  • So far this page names 436 of the 441 council and regional seats in these 42 cities. The rest are added as cities post their full results.

The words on this page

  • Ward councillor: elected by the voters of one part of the city, called a ward. Some cities, such as Chatham-Kent, Guelph and Welland, elect two or more per ward.
  • At-large councillor: elected by the whole city, not one ward. North Bay, Cornwall and Niagara Falls elect their whole council this way.
  • Regional councillor: in a city that belongs to a region (Peel, York, Durham, Halton, Waterloo), sits on both the city council and the regional council, which runs things like police, transit, water and social services for the whole region.
  • Acclaimed: only one person ran for the seat, so they won without a vote.
  • Unofficial results: the count announced on election night. The city clerk declares the official results a few days later.

Common questions

Are these the official results?

Not yet. These are the unofficial results announced on election night. Each city clerk declares the official results in the days after the vote, and we update this page when they do.

When do the new mayors and councils start?

The new four-year term of every Ontario municipal council begins on November 15, 2026.

What happens when a race is very close?

A tie leads to a recount. A city council can also order a recount, some cities have a rule that triggers one automatically when the gap is very small, and a voter can ask a judge for one.

Why are some names missing?

Some cities post their results in a format that is slow to read, and a few races were too close to call. We only add a name once the city's own results page shows it, or two separate news reports agree.
