🎨 Color Match

Tap the COLOUR of the word — not what it says!

A colour word appears — but it's printed in a different colour! Your job is to tap the button that matches the colour of the letters, not the word you read. For example, if the word "RED" is printed in blue, you tap BLUE. It's trickier than it sounds! Answer as many as you can before time runs out. Every right answer adds a little time.
