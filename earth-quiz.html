About this quiz

The Earth and Environment Quiz is a gentle science quiz about the planet we live on. It asks about rocks and volcanoes, clouds and storms, oceans and rivers, forests, wildlife, waste and electricity, and it explains every answer in one short sentence.

It was written for children who are curious about nature, and for parents or teachers looking for a calm ten minute activity. There is no timer and no wrong way to play, because a mistake simply turns into something new that you have learned.

What's inside

  • Six topics to choose from: Our Planet Earth, Weather and Climate, Oceans and Water, Forests and Wildlife, Recycling and Waste, and Energy and Saving Power.
  • Each round gives you ten questions with four answer choices, and the correct one lights up the moment you tap.
  • A short explanation appears under every question, so you finish the round knowing why an answer was right.
  • A question counter and a progress bar show where you are in the round and how many points you hold.
  • The question bank holds about one hundred questions, so the same ten rarely come around twice in a row.
  • When the round ends you can pick another topic right away or move on to a different quiz.

Good to know

  • Roughly seven tenths of Earth's surface is covered by water, and about 97 parts in 100 of that water is salty ocean.
  • Only about one tenth of an iceberg shows above the waterline, which is why ships give them a very wide berth.
  • Water molecules lock together in a six sided pattern, so almost every snowflake grows exactly six arms.
  • Thunder is the sound of air exploding outward after a lightning bolt heats it in a fraction of a second.
  • The Canadian Shield, wrapped around Hudson Bay, is made of some of the oldest rock found anywhere on the planet.
  • Lake Superior is the largest of the Great Lakes by surface area, and it is the deepest of them as well.
