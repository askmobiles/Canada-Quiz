About this quiz

The General Knowledge Quiz is the largest quiz on the site: 1,350 questions arranged into nine subjects and three difficulty levels. You choose a level first, then a subject, and answer ten questions with a short explanation after each one.

Easy is written for ages seven and up, Medium suits teenagers and adults, and Hard is a genuine challenge. Because one page serves a whole family at three different levels, it works as well at a kitchen table as it does in a classroom.

What's inside

  • Nine subjects: World and Geography, Science and Nature, Math and Numbers, Animals, History, Sports, Technology, Words Art and Music, and Fun Facts.
  • Three level buttons sit above the subjects, and the level you picked shows on a pill throughout the round.
  • Every subject holds about fifty questions at each of the three levels, which is how the total reaches 1,350.
  • A round is ten questions, and each explanation adds a detail rather than simply repeating the answer.
  • The results screen offers a replay, a step up to the next level, and a way back to the subject list.

Good to know

  • The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific drops almost eleven kilometres, which is deeper than Mount Everest is tall.
  • Lesotho is completely surrounded by South Africa, and a country ringed by a single neighbour is called an enclave.
  • Lake Baikal in Siberia is over 1,600 metres deep and holds more fresh water than any other lake on Earth.
  • Istanbul straddles the Bosphorus strait, which places one half of the city in Europe and the other half in Asia.
  • Michelangelo carved his statue of David from a single marble block that other sculptors had already rejected as flawed.
  • The phrase to bury the hatchet comes from an Indigenous North American custom of burying weapons once peace was agreed.

Common questions

Which difficulty level should I begin with?

Try Medium first if you are an adult. If several questions in a row feel obvious, step up to Hard from the results screen; if they feel heavy, drop to Easy and build up.

Can I play the same subject more than once?

Yes. Each subject holds about fifty questions per level while a round shows ten, so replaying the same subject at the same level still mixes the selection up.

Does this quiz follow a school curriculum?

The subjects overlap with school topics such as geography, science, history and arithmetic, but the quiz follows no official syllabus. Treat it as practice and general reading, not as coursework.

Do I need an account to keep my score?

No account exists on this site at all. Your score appears at the end of each round and is then cleared, which keeps the game quick and keeps your details out of it.
