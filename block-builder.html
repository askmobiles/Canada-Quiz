🍁 Canada Block World

Dig, build and wander a whole world made of blocks — in 3D, in bright colours, in your browser, with nothing to install.

How to play

Press Play, then look around and start digging. Nothing can hurt you and nothing is timed, so there is no wrong way to do it.

Try this first: dig three blocks into a hillside, put a plank floor down, glass at the front, and a snow roof on top. That is a cabin, and it takes about two minutes.

What you can do

The surfaces are deliberately smooth rather than pixelated, drawn at high resolution so they stay clean on a big screen, and every corner where two blocks meet is shaded a little darker — which is what makes a world of cubes look solid instead of flat.

The world is a small Canadian landscape: rolling ground with grass and stone underneath, sandy shores around the water, birch and maple trees you can chop, and snow on the higher ground. Everything you can see is made of blocks, and every one of them can be dug out or put back.

You can change how the world looks with the Style button under it. Smooth is the default: soft matte surfaces in muted colours. Bright is the one for younger builders, with bolder colours and stronger shading. Frosty turns the whole world to winter.

Twelve block types sit in the bar along the bottom — grass, dirt, stone, sand, wood, planks, leaves, red maple leaves, snow, ice, glass and brick. Glass and ice let the light through, so you can build a window and still see out.

There is no health, no monsters and nothing to lose. It is a quiet building game: make a cabin, dig a mine, build an inukshuk out of stone, or flatten a hill just to see how long it takes. Turning flying on lets younger builders get above their work without falling off it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The controls change to a thumb stick and buttons, and the world is deliberately small so it stays smooth on a school tablet or an older phone. If it does run slowly, closing other browser tabs usually fixes it — a 3D world is the most demanding thing on this site by a long way.

It needs WebGL, which every browser from the last decade supports. If you see a message saying 3D is unavailable, it is usually hardware acceleration switched off in the browser settings.

Common questions

Is this Minecraft?

No, and it is not connected to it in any way. Minecraft is made by Mojang and owned by Microsoft. This is our own small block-building game, written from scratch, with every texture drawn in code rather than copied from anywhere.

Does it need to be installed?

No. It runs in the browser page you are already on. There is nothing to download, no account and no sign-up.

Where is my world saved?

In this browser, on this device, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears the world, and a different device starts fresh.

It runs slowly on my phone. Can I fix it?

Closing other browser tabs usually helps most. A 3D world is by far the most demanding thing on this site, so an older phone will run it more slowly than the quizzes and the other games.
