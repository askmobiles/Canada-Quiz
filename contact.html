About this page

This is where you send a message about Canada Quiz. A correction to a quiz answer, an idea for a subject we have not covered, or a note that a game misbehaved on your phone are all genuinely welcome here.

Everything arrives at one email address. There is no form to fill in, no captcha to squint at and no account to create — write from whichever mailbox you already use.

This is an independent site with no connection to the Government of Canada. That distinction matters most when your question is about a real application rather than a page on this site.

What you'll find here

  • The contact email address is shown in full, with a button that opens a new message in your own mail app.
  • A short section explains how to report a wrong answer: name the quiz and quote the question exactly as you saw it.
  • A clear notice lists what cannot be handled here and points to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada for real cases.
  • The footer carries links to the privacy policy and the terms if your question concerns data or rules of use.

Worth remembering

  • Corrections are acted on faster when they name the exact page and repeat the wording, because several quizzes cover overlapping topics.
  • Nobody here can look up an immigration file, check the status of an application, or give legal advice about an individual case.
  • Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada is the federal department responsible for real citizenship applications, and its website is linked from this page.
  • One clear message with all the details is easier to act on than several short ones.

Common questions

What sort of messages belong on this page?

Anything about the website itself: a factual error in a quiz, a game that will not load, a broken link, or a request for a topic you would like added.

Can you help me with my own citizenship application?

Unfortunately not, and the page says so plainly. This is a study and games website with no access to any government system. Questions about your file must go to the federal department.
