🧊 Cube Twist

Scramble it, twist the faces, and solve the 3D cube!

📖 How to play

Press Scramble to mix up the cube. On a phone or tablet you can play with your finger: swipe straight across the cube and the row you swiped turns that way — up, down, left or right, just like a real cube. To look at the other sides, drag the empty space around the cube. You can also use the letter buttons to turn each face: U (up), D (down), L (left), R (right), F (front), B (back), with Reverse ↺ to turn a face the other way. Make every face a single solid colour to win! Tip: the middle square of each face never moves — that's the colour that side should become.

About this game

The Cube Twist puzzle on this page is a real 3D cube drawn right in your browser. You can turn its faces, spin it around to look at the back, and try to bring every side back to one solid colour. Nothing to download, nothing to install.

Solving a cube for the first time takes patience, and that is the point. Kids who like puzzles, teenagers who want a party trick, and adults who owned a plastic cube years ago all end up here for the same quiet satisfaction of the last twist.

How it works

  • The scramble button mixes the cube with twenty-five random turns, so no two puzzles ever start the same way.
  • On a phone or tablet, swipe straight across the cube and the row under your finger turns that way.
  • Six letter buttons turn each face: U for up, D for down, then L, R, F and B.
  • Tick the reverse box to send any face the opposite way, or hold Shift with the keyboard letters.
  • A move counter and a timer run while you work, and one button puts the cube back to solved.
  • Arrow buttons tilt your view, and a reset returns the cube to its starting angle.

Tips for playing

  • The middle square of each face never moves, no matter how you turn the cube. That square tells you which colour that whole side must become.
  • There are eight corner pieces and twelve edge pieces. A corner always stays a corner, so a piece with three colours can never sit on an edge.
  • Most solvers work layer by layer: the bottom cross first, then the bottom corners, then the middle band, leaving the top face for last.
  • Turning the same face four times brings it back to where it started, which is a handy way to undo a mistake without panicking.
  • Speedcubers count moves as well as seconds, so a slow, tidy solution that uses fewer turns is still an impressive result.

Common questions

Do I need to know a solving method to enjoy this?

Not at all. Plenty of people simply scramble the cube and keep turning faces until things look better. If you want to go further, finishing one whole layer before touching the rest is the usual next step.

Can I play with a mouse instead of a finger?

Yes. Drag with the mouse the same way you would swipe, or click the six letter buttons instead. The keyboard letters U, D, L, R, F and B work too, with Shift for a reverse turn.

Does the page save my best time?

No. The timer and the move counter belong to the puzzle in front of you and reset whenever you scramble again, so every attempt begins clean and nothing is stored.

What happens if I get stuck halfway through?

You can keep twisting for as long as you like, or use the reset to put every face back in order and start a fresh scramble. There is no penalty and no time limit anywhere.
