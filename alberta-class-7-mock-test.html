About this quiz

This page copies the shape of the Alberta Class 7 knowledge test so the real one feels familiar. You answer fifteen road sign questions and fifteen rules of the road questions, thirty in all, with a thirty-minute timer running the whole way through.

The pass mark is twenty-five out of thirty, counted across the whole test rather than half by half. That is gentler than Ontario, where each half must reach its own mark, but it still leaves you room for only five mistakes in total.

The questions are drawn at random from our bank of one hundred and twenty original questions, so no two runs look the same. This is unofficial practice with no link to the Government of Alberta or any registry agent, and passing here gives you confidence rather than a licence.

What's inside

  • The test runs fifteen sign questions and fifteen rules questions, the same balance the real knowledge test uses.
  • A thirty-minute timer counts down on screen, and the test ends the moment it reaches zero.
  • Every answer is marked the instant you tap it, with a short explanation of why that choice is the right one.
  • The result screen shows your total out of thirty and a clear PASS or TRY AGAIN against the mark of twenty-five.
  • Every question you missed is listed at the end with the answer you picked, the correct answer and the reason behind it.
  • Questions are picked at random from sixty sign questions and sixty rules questions, so every run is a fresh test.

Good to know

  • Five wrong answers is your whole budget, no matter which part of the test they fall in.
  • Because Alberta totals the whole test, a very strong showing on the signs can quietly rescue a shaky patch on the rules.
  • Thirty minutes for thirty questions gives you a full minute each, which is plenty once the material is familiar.
  • When two choices both sound sensible, the safer and more patient one is usually what the guide expects.
  • Run the test several times, because the random draw will show you questions an earlier attempt never reached.

Common questions

Is this the official Alberta Class 7 knowledge test?

No. This is unofficial practice written in our own words, and we are not affiliated with the Government of Alberta or any registry agent. A pass here is a good sign of readiness, nothing more.

How is the score counted on this mock test?

Your thirty answers are totalled together and compared with the pass mark of twenty-five, exactly as Alberta does it. There is no separate mark for the sign half and the rules half.

What happens if the timer reaches zero before I finish?

The test stops where you are and takes you straight to your result. Questions you never opened are counted as wrong, so it is better to keep moving than to sit on one difficult question.

Can I look back at the questions I answered wrongly?

Yes. The result screen gathers every missed question in one list, showing what you chose, what was correct and a short explanation. That list tells you which chapters or which sign groups to go back to.

Do I need an account before I start the Alberta mock test?

No account, no email and nothing to install. The test begins in your browser as soon as the page loads, and you can close the tab and start over whenever you have half an hour free.
