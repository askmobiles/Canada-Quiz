What the real Manitoba knowledge test looks like

Manitoba Public Insurance publishes less about its test than most provinces. What MPI does state is concrete: the test is taken on a computer, it is not open book, and there is a 30-minute limit. It covers traffic laws, safe driving practices and recognising road signs. Tests are available in English, French and more than twenty other languages.

MPI does not publish the number of questions or the pass mark. You will see 40 questions and 80 percent quoted confidently on other practice sites; none of them cites MPI, and they contradict each other on whether that 80 percent is per section or across the whole test. We are not going to guess at a number that decides whether someone books a test. Our mock test gives you 30 questions in 30 minutes, matching the one timing MPI does publish.

The rest of the process is well documented. You can start at 16, or at 15 and a half if you are enrolled in MPI's Driver Z programme at a high school. You need identification and must be registered as an MPI customer before booking, and you must meet the vision standards. The knowledge test costs $12. After passing you spend at least nine months at the learner stage and at least fifteen months at the intermediate stage before a full Class 5. Your supervising driver must have been fully licensed for 36 months. If you fail, learners wait seven days before trying again.

How to pass on your first try

Read the official guide once from start to finish, then use practice questions to find your weak spots. Most people lose marks on the signs, not the rules, because there are a lot of signs and many look similar. Study the signs in small groups rather than all at once, and pay attention to shape and colour: a red circle with a line through it always means you must not do something, a yellow diamond always warns you about what is coming, and an orange sign always means road work.

Take the mock test more than once. The questions are picked at random each time, so you will see a different test on every run. When you can pass three times in a row, you are ready to book the real one.

Worth remembering

  • Manitoba Public Insurance gives you thirty minutes for the knowledge test, and it covers three areas: traffic laws, safe driving practices and road sign recognition. The test is taken on a computer and it is not open book.
  • The learner stage lasts a minimum of nine months before you can move to the intermediate stage.
  • Your supervising driver must have held a full licence for at least thirty-six months, must be under .05 blood alcohol, and sits in the front passenger seat.
  • Because the supervisor takes the front seat, any other passengers ride in the rear — one for each working seatbelt.

Common questions

How many questions are on the real Manitoba test?

Manitoba Public Insurance publishes the thirty-minute limit and the three subject areas, but not the exact question count or pass mark. Rather than invent one, our mock test uses a clearly stated approximation and the real thirty-minute clock.

What is the minimum age in Manitoba?

Sixteen, or fifteen and a half if you are enrolled in a high school driver education course.

Are these the real MPI questions?

No. Every question here is written in our own words from the free Manitoba Driver's Handbook. This is an unofficial practice site with no connection to MPI or the Province of Manitoba.
