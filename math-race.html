About this game

Math Race is timed mental arithmetic dressed up as a canoe race. You choose the kind of question, the grade level and the length of the round, then answer as fast as you can while a canoe paddles across the track towards a chequered flag.

It is built for practice that actually sticks. Answers are typed on a number pad and confirmed with Enter, so a mistyped digit can be fixed with the backspace key instead of costing you a question you really did know.

Parents and teachers like it because the end screen is honest. It lists every question you missed beside the right answer, so a child can see the exact facts to work on rather than a single unhelpful score.

How it works

  • Five modes are offered: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and a mixed practice that shuffles all four.
  • Three levels match school years, Grade 1-2, Grade 3-4 and Grade 5-6, and the numbers grow with each step.
  • Rounds last either sixty or one hundred and twenty seconds, chosen before the race begins.
  • At Grade 5-6 only, a wrong answer costs two seconds off the clock, which rewards care over wild guessing.
  • The canoe advances with every correct answer and earns a chequered lap marker for each ten you get right.
  • The results screen shows correct answers, mistakes, accuracy, questions per minute, best streak and your personal best.

Tips for playing

  • Grade 1-2 addition keeps every total at twenty or less, which is exactly the range children are asked to know by heart at that age.
  • Grade 1-2 multiplication sticks to the two, five and ten times tables, the three that most children learn before all the others.
  • Division questions are built backwards from a whole-number answer, so the result always divides evenly and no remainders ever appear.
  • Your personal best is stored separately for each combination of operation, level and round length, so switching modes never wipes a record.
  • Streaks are worth chasing. Answering steadily beats rushing, because one careless slip breaks a run you spent half the round building.
  • Read the questions per minute number as your speed and the accuracy number as your care. Improving one at the cost of the other is not progress.

Common questions

Do I have to press Enter after every answer?

Yes, and that is on purpose. Nothing is judged until you confirm, so a child who taps the wrong digit can clear it with the backspace key and still get the question right.

Which level suits a child who is just starting?

Begin at Grade 1-2 with a sixty second round and one single operation. Move up only when the accuracy score sits comfortably high, because speed on shaky facts becomes a habit that is hard to fix.

Are my scores sent anywhere?

No. Your personal best is kept in your own browser on your own device, and there is no account, no name to enter and no class list. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Can I stop a round before the timer runs out?

Yes, an End the round button finishes it straight away and takes you to the results, including the list of questions you missed. From there you can play again or change your settings.

Is the mixed practice mode harder than the others?

It is harder in a useful way. Numbers stay at the level you picked, but you never know which of the four operations is coming, so you must read each question properly instead of running on autopilot.
