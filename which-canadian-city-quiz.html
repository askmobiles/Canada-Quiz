Which Canadian City Should You Live In?

Seven questions about weather, weekends and rent, and one city that fits the way you actually live.

📖 How to play

Tap the choice that feels most like you. There are seven questions, no right answers and nothing to study. At the end you get one result, plus the one that came second, and a button to run the whole thing again.

About this quiz

Seven practical questions — winter, rent, commute, how far wilderness should be — and one Canadian city at the end. It is a personality quiz, not advice, so treat the answer as a conversation starter rather than a plan.

The questions are the ones people actually argue about when they think of moving, which is why the quiz works as well for someone who has lived here all their life as for a newcomer choosing a first city.

Seven cities appear as results, spread from the Pacific to the Atlantic, and each one comes with a short description of the life that goes with it.

What's inside

  • Seven questions with four choices each and a progress bar.
  • Seven results: Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary, Halifax, Winnipeg and St. John's.
  • The questions cover winter, weekends, rent, commuting, wilderness, small talk and what you want more of.
  • A Try again button restarts it at once.
  • No signup, nothing saved, works on a phone.

Good to know

  • Toronto is the largest city in Canada and one of the most linguistically varied cities in the world.
  • Montreal sits on an island in the St. Lawrence River and is the largest mainly French-speaking city in the Americas.
  • Vancouver has the mildest winters of any large Canadian city, which is also why it rains so much.
  • Calgary sits about a thousand metres above sea level and is roughly an hour and a half from Banff National Park.
  • St. John's is one of the oldest European-founded settlements in North America and its harbour is reached through a narrow gap in the cliffs.

Common questions

Should I actually move because of this?

No. It is a game. Moving depends on work, family, immigration status, housing and a dozen other things this quiz knows nothing about.

Why is my city not one of the answers?

Seven results keep it quick. The seven were picked to cover very different climates and lifestyles from coast to coast rather than to rank anywhere.

How long does it take?

About a minute.

Can I get a different city?

Yes. Change two answers and the totals shift, which is half the fun of running it twice.
