Nunavut Class 7 Mock Test

Two parts, sign identification and the rules of the road, with fifteen right needed out of twenty in each and forty minutes on the clock. Every one of those numbers is our own practice standard, because Nunavut publishes no question count, no pass mark and no time limit. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on. At the end you get your score for each half, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

When you are ready to book the real one

Take this three times on different days and look at where the misses land rather than at the total. Because the territory publishes no pass mark, the useful question is not what percentage you scored but whether the same thing is catching you twice. Working one part at a time also matches how the real examination is rewritten, since failing one part means rewriting only that part.

There is no online knowledge test in Nunavut and no online booking page, so you go in person to a Motor Vehicle service provider with your documents. Three Motor Vehicles Offices serve the whole territory, at Iqaluit, Rankin Inlet and Gjoa Haven, and every other community is served through the Government Liaison Officer programme, with a Government Liaison Office in all twenty-five communities, and through third-party examiners the department has been adding across the territory. In 2023 the territory recorded 3,349 knowledge examinations and 1,074 road tests, so this is a well-travelled path even without a highway between communities.

Where to get the official information

Everything on this page comes from what Nunavut publishes for free. The Nunavut Driver's Manual is the book the knowledge test is written from, and it is free to download from the territory's own site. It is published in English only, as are the territory's three other driver manuals, even though the Government of Nunavut website itself is available in Inuktitut, Inuinnaqtun, English and French. That is why the English title appears in the French place on this site: there is no French edition to name. Read the manual alongside this practice, not instead of it.

This is an unofficial free practice site. It is not affiliated with the Motor Vehicles Division, the Department of Economic Development and Transportation or the Government of Nunavut, and it cannot issue a licence. Always check the official pages for the current rules before you book.

About this quiz

This is the timed version. Two parts are drawn at random from the Nunavut bank, sign identification and the rules of the road, and each part is scored on its own, the way the real examination is rewritten one part at a time.

The question count, the pass mark and the timer are ours, not the territory's. Nunavut publishes none of the three, and inventing one would be exactly the kind of confident wrong number this site exists to avoid.

Because the questions are picked at random each time, every run is a different test. Take it on three separate days before you decide you are ready.

What's inside

  • Twenty sign questions and twenty rules questions, drawn at random from the sixty-question bank.
  • Fifteen correct answers needed in each part, which is our practice standard and not the territory's.
  • A forty-minute countdown, clearly labelled as our practice standard rather than a published rule.
  • A short explanation after every answer, right or wrong.
  • A pass or try again result for each part, scored separately.
  • A list of every question you missed, so you know which page to go back to.

Good to know

  • Nunavut publishes no question count, no pass mark and no time limit, so treat every number here as practice pressure rather than a rule.
  • In the real examination, failing one part means rewriting only that part, which is why the two halves here are scored separately.
  • The knowledge test is not offered online and there is no online booking page, so you attend a Motor Vehicle service provider in person.
  • A Nunavut licence is issued for five years, and a licence expired more than twelve months cannot be renewed without being examined again.
  • In 2023 the territory recorded 3,349 knowledge examinations and 1,074 road tests, and issued 774 Class 7 licences.

Common questions

Is this the real Nunavut test?

No. It is free practice written for this site. The territory publishes no format at all, so the number of questions, the pass mark and the timer are all ours and we label them that way.

Why are the two parts scored separately?

Because that is how the real examination works. If you fail one part you rewrite only that part, and scoring them apart here shows you which one to study.

Can I take it more than once?

As often as you like. The questions are drawn at random each time, so the test is different on every run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for, and there is a full-screen mode for a phone or a tablet.

Where do I go if I fail a part?

Back to the study page for that part — the road signs page or the rules of the road page — and then take this again.
