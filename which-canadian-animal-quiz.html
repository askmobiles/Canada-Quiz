Which Canadian Animal Are You?

Seven quick questions and you will know which Canadian animal matches the way you live.

📖 How to play

Tap the choice that feels most like you. There are seven questions, no right answers and nothing to study. At the end you get one result, plus the one that came second, and a button to run the whole thing again.

About this quiz

Seven light questions, one animal at the end. This is a personality quiz rather than a nature test, so nothing here is right or wrong and there is nothing to revise.

The questions ask where you would spend an afternoon, how you get through a Canadian winter, what you do at a party and what people thank you for. Each answer quietly adds points to different animals, and the highest total is your result.

It takes about a minute and it is made for sharing. Families play it together and compare results, and it is a friendly way for newcomers to meet the animals they will keep seeing on coins, flags and road signs.

What's inside

  • Seven questions, four choices each, with a progress bar so you can see how far along you are.
  • Seven possible results: the beaver, the moose, the loon, the polar bear, the orca, the snowy owl and the Canada goose.
  • Every result comes with a short description of the character behind it, not just the name of an animal.
  • A Try again button restarts the quiz at once, so you can see how one different answer changes the outcome.
  • The whole thing runs in the page with no signup, no account and nothing saved anywhere.

Good to know

  • The beaver has been an official emblem of Canada since 1975 and appears on the five-cent coin.
  • The common loon is on the one-dollar coin, which is where the nickname loonie comes from.
  • A bull moose can stand about two metres at the shoulder, making it the tallest land animal in the country.
  • The snowy owl is the official bird of Quebec and hunts in daylight, unlike most other owls.
  • Orcas live in family groups that stay together for life, and the same pods return to the British Columbia coast year after year.

Common questions

Is there a right answer in this quiz?

No. Every choice adds points to different animals, so no answer can be wrong. Two people can pick opposite things and both get a result that suits them.

How long does it take?

About a minute. Seven questions, one tap each, so it fits into a coffee break.

Can I get a different animal next time?

Yes. Press Try again and change two answers and the totals shift. Many people run it twice on purpose to see which animal comes second.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup and nothing is stored. Close the page and the result is gone.
