Which Winter Sport Are You?

Seven questions about cold mornings, teamwork and how much you like falling over.

📖 How to play

Tap the choice that feels most like you. There are seven questions, no right answers and nothing to study. At the end you get one result, plus the one that came second, and a button to run the whole thing again.

About this quiz

Seven questions about cold mornings, gear budgets and how much you mind falling over, and one winter sport at the end. There is nothing to get right, so nobody has to have played any of them.

The seven results deliberately mix the expensive with the free. Downhill skiing needs a lift ticket, tobogganing needs a hill and a plastic sled, and both count as a proper Canadian winter.

It takes about a minute and works well as a family game in February, when everybody has an opinion about the cold.

What's inside

  • Seven questions, four choices each, with a progress bar.
  • Seven results: hockey, figure skating, downhill skiing, snowboarding, curling, snowshoeing and tobogganing.
  • Each result describes the temperament behind the sport, not the rules of it.
  • A Try again button restarts the quiz instantly.
  • Free, no signup, and it fits on a phone screen.

Good to know

  • Curling arrived in Canada with Scottish soldiers in the late 1700s and the country now has more curlers than anywhere else.
  • Ice hockey and lacrosse are both recognised in Canadian law as national sports, hockey for winter and lacrosse for summer.
  • Snowshoes were developed by Indigenous peoples across the North long before Europeans arrived, and the modern shape still copies them.
  • Canada has hosted the Winter Olympics twice, in Calgary in 1988 and in Vancouver in 2010.
  • The Rideau Canal Skateway in Ottawa freezes into one of the largest naturally frozen skating surfaces anywhere, when the winter is cold enough.

Common questions

Do I need to have played these sports?

Not at all. The questions are about how you like to spend a cold day, not about skill.

How long does it take?

About a minute, seven questions.

Is any result better than another?

No. Tobogganing is free and takes nine seconds, and it counts exactly as much as anything else here.

Can I get a different sport?

Yes, change a couple of answers and press Try again.
