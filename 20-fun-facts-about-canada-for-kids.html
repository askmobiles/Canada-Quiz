🎈 20 Fun Facts About Canada for Kids

Twenty fun and surprising facts about Canada for kids and families — from polar bears to poutine to the longest coastline in the world.

Canada is a country full of surprises! Here are 20 fun facts that kids and grown-ups will love. How many did you already know?

  • Canada is the second-largest country in the whole world by land area.
  • Canada has the longest coastline of any country on Earth.
  • There are more lakes in Canada than in all the rest of the world combined.
  • Canada has two official languages: English and French.
  • The capital city of Canada is Ottawa.
  • The maple leaf is Canada's most famous symbol.
  • Canada is home to about half of the world's polar bears.
  • The town of Churchill, Manitoba, is called the “Polar Bear Capital of the World.”
  • Hockey and lacrosse are Canada's official national sports.
  • Poutine — fries with cheese curds and gravy — was invented in Quebec.
  • Basketball was invented by a Canadian named James Naismith.
  • Canada has 10 provinces and 3 territories.
  • The world-famous Niagara Falls is on the border between Canada and the United States.
  • Canada's money includes coins nicknamed the “loonie” and the “toonie.”
  • The beaver is an official symbol of Canada.
  • Canada has six time zones.
  • The Canadian Rockies have beautiful mountains, lakes, and glaciers.
  • Canada Day is celebrated every year on July 1st.
  • Insulin, which helps people with diabetes, was discovered in Canada.
  • Santa Claus has a Canadian postal code: H0H 0H0!
