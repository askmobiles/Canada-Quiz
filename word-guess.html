🔤 Canada Word Guess

Guess the Canadian word one letter at a time. You have 6 tries!

📖 How to play

A hidden Canadian word is shown as blanks. Tap letters (or use your keyboard) to guess. Correct letters fill in; each wrong guess costs a maple leaf life. Guess the whole word before your 6 lives run out!

About this game

Canada Word Guess is the old paper game of hangman, redrawn with maple leaves. A Canadian word hides behind a row of blanks and you uncover it one letter at a time, with six leaves standing between you and a lost round.

Most of the words belong to Canada in some way, since animals, cities, food, weather, money and clothing all appear. A short hint sits above the blanks, so even someone who has just arrived in the country has something to work from.

It is quick, quiet and easy to share around a table. Children tap the letter buttons, adults use the keyboard, and a running wins counter always gives you a reason to try just one more word.

How it works

  • A hint pill above the puzzle describes the hidden word, such as fries with cheese curds and gravy.
  • Six maple leaves stand for your lives, and each one turns into a broken heart when you guess wrong.
  • A full alphabet of buttons sits below the word, and each letter dims once you have already tried it.
  • Correct letters turn the button green and fill into the blanks, while wrong letters turn it red and cost a life.
  • A wins counter climbs each time you solve a word, and the New Word button starts a fresh puzzle instantly.
  • You can play by touching the on-screen letters or by typing them on a real keyboard, and both behave identically.

Tips for playing

  • Start with vowels. Most of the words hide an A, an E or an O somewhere, and finding one quickly narrows the shape of the answer.
  • After the vowels, try the common consonants R, S, T, N and L before spending a life on a rare letter like Z.
  • Read the hint carefully, because it names the category, so a clue about a warm winter hat points straight at a short word.
  • Count the blanks. A six-letter word with a hint about the capital city leaves you very few possibilities left to test.
  • If you lose, the whole word is revealed. Lost rounds teach you the word list, so later games get noticeably easier.

Common questions

How many wrong guesses am I allowed?

Six. Each mistake removes one maple leaf. When the last leaf goes the round ends and the page shows you the word you were hunting for.

Are all the words about Canada?

Nearly all of them, covering places, animals, food, coins and winter things. A few general words appear too, so the list is not only about geography.

Is this useful practice for learning English?

It can be. The words are short, common and paired with a plain hint, which makes it a gentle way to build everyday vocabulary for newcomers and children.

Can I play without a keyboard?

Yes. Every letter has its own button on screen, sized for fingers, so a phone or tablet works perfectly well with no typing at all.

Does the game get harder as I win?

No, the difficulty stays level. Words are drawn at random each round, so a very short one can follow a very long one with no warning.
