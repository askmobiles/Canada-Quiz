🏔️ Canada's Geography: From Coast to Coast to Coast

Explore Canada's geography — its oceans, mountains, prairies, lakes, and regions. A simple guide for students and newcomers.

Canada is a huge country with many different landscapes. People often say it stretches “from coast to coast to coast,” because it touches three oceans. Let's take a quick tour of Canada's geography.

Three oceans

Canada is bordered by the Atlantic Ocean in the east, the Pacific Ocean in the west, and the Arctic Ocean in the north. That is why the country is described as coast to coast to coast.

The main regions

  • The Atlantic provinces in the east, with fishing towns and rocky coasts.
  • Central Canada (Ontario and Quebec), where most people live and many big cities are found.
  • The Prairies, wide flat lands great for farming.
  • The West Coast (British Columbia), with mountains, forests, and the Pacific Ocean.
  • The North (the three territories), a cold and beautiful land near the Arctic.

Amazing geography facts

  • Canada is the second-largest country in the world by land area.
  • It has the longest coastline of any country.
  • Canada has more lakes than any other country.
  • The Canadian Rockies have tall mountains and stunning glaciers.
  • Canada shares the longest border in the world with the United States.
