🍁 Rights and Respect

Game

Ten things that happen in a Canadian schoolyard, a classroom and a street in winter. Choose what should happen, and find out which right or which responsibility it comes from. For ages 7 to 12.

Advertisement

For the grown-up sitting beside them

Every answer here comes from two official sources and nowhere else: the Department of Justice on the rights and freedoms the Charter protects, and Discover Canada, the official citizenship study guide, for the responsibilities it names — obeying the law, taking responsibility for yourself and your family, serving on a jury, voting, helping others in the community, and protecting and enjoying our heritage and environment.

One thing is said carefully throughout. The Charter binds governments, not children in a schoolyard, so a child who will not pass the ball is not breaking the Constitution. The answers talk about what Canada promises and what Canadians do, never about what is illegal — while the facts that really are legal facts are exact: you vote at eighteen, both languages are official, and you may live anywhere in the country.
