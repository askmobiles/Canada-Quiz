💡 Lights Out

Turn every light off. Sounds easy — tapping one light also flips the ones beside it.

📖 How to play

Tap any square. That square switches on or off, and so do the squares directly above, below, left and right of it. Your job is to get the whole board dark. Two things are worth knowing. First, tapping the same square twice puts everything back exactly as it was, so nothing is ever ruined. Second, the order of your taps does not matter at all — only which squares you tap, and whether you tap each one an odd or an even number of times. About this game

Lights Out is a logic puzzle that looks like nothing and then keeps you up. Every square is a light. Tap one and it switches, and so do the squares directly above, below, left and right of it. The goal is a completely dark board.

Two facts make it fair rather than frustrating. Tapping the same square twice undoes itself completely, so you can never wreck a puzzle. And the order of your taps makes no difference at all — only which squares you tapped, and whether you tapped each an odd or an even number of times.

Every puzzle is created by starting from a dark board and pressing random squares, so a solution always exists — you are never given something impossible. Three sizes: three by three to learn on, four by four to think about, and the classic five by five, which is genuinely difficult. Your fewest moves at each size is remembered in your browser.

Tips for playing

  • Because order does not matter, no square ever needs to be tapped more than once in a solution. That alone cuts the search enormously.
  • A common method is to clear the board row by row from the top, tapping the row below whichever lights are still on. On a five by five board this leaves a bottom row that tells you what to do first.

Common questions

Can a puzzle be impossible?

Not here. Every board is made by scrambling a solved one, so it can always be unscrambled. On a randomly filled five by five board that would not be true.

Does the number of moves matter?

Only for your own record. Finishing is finishing, but your best is saved so you can try to beat it.
