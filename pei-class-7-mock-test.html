Prince Edward Island Class 7 Mock Test

Two halves, signs and rules, with twelve right needed out of fifteen in each and thirty minutes on the clock. Every one of those numbers is our own practice standard, because Prince Edward Island publishes no question count, no pass mark and no time limit. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on. At the end you get your score for each half, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

When you are ready to book the real one

Take this three times on different days and look at where the misses land rather than at the total. If they cluster in the signs, go back to the sign page; if they cluster in the rules, go back to the rules page. Because the Province publishes no pass mark, the useful question is not what percentage you scored but whether anything is still catching you twice.

When you are ready, you book by contacting an Access PEI location for a written knowledge exam appointment, and you take a vision test at the same visit. Students in a certified driver-education program write the test in class, but they still attend Access PEI for the vision test. If the written exam does not go your way, the Province publishes a wait of at least seven days before you rewrite it and no fee to rewrite.

Where to get the official information

Everything on this page comes from what Prince Edward Island publishes for free. The Driver's Handbook is the book the written test is based on, it is free to download from the Province's own site, and it is published in French, Arabic and Chinese as well as English. Whether the test itself is offered in those languages is not published anywhere, so we do not claim that it is. Read the handbook alongside this practice, not instead of it.

This is an unofficial free practice site. It is not affiliated with Access PEI, the Highway Safety Division or the Government of Prince Edward Island, and it cannot issue a licence. Always check the official pages for the current rules and fees before you book.

About this quiz

This is the timed version. Two halves are drawn at random from the Prince Edward Island bank, signs and rules, and each half is scored on its own.

The question count, the pass mark and the timer are ours, not the Province's. Prince Edward Island publishes none of the three, and inventing one would be exactly the kind of confident wrong number this site exists to avoid.

Because the questions are picked at random each time, every run is a different test. Take it on three separate days before you decide you are ready.

What's inside

  • Fifteen road sign questions and fifteen rules questions, drawn at random from the sixty-question bank.
  • Twelve correct answers needed in each half, which is our practice standard and not the Province's.
  • A thirty-minute countdown, clearly labelled as our practice standard rather than a published rule.
  • A short explanation after every answer, right or wrong.
  • A pass or try again result for each half, scored separately.
  • A list of every question you missed, so you know which page to go back to.

Good to know

  • Prince Edward Island publishes no question count, no pass mark and no time limit for its written test, so treat every number here as practice pressure rather than a rule.
  • The instruction permit itself is valid for two years, and the Province publishes a fee of twenty dollars for the permit and another for the permit test.
  • The Province does not publish how long a passed written test stays valid.
  • A failed written exam carries a published wait of at least seven days, and a failed road test a wait of at least fourteen.
  • Stage 1 lasts at least 365 days, or 275 days with a certified driver-education program, and the whole graduated programme takes a minimum of three years.

Common questions

Is this the real Prince Edward Island test?

No. It is free practice written for this site. The Province publishes no format at all, so the number of questions, the pass mark and the timer are all ours and we label them that way.

Why are the two halves scored separately?

Because it shows you where the weakness is. A total can hide a bad half, and going back to the right study page is the whole point.

Can I take it more than once?

As often as you like. The questions are drawn at random each time, so the test is different on every run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for, and there is a full-screen mode for a phone or a tablet.

Where do I go if I fail a half?

Back to the study page for that half — the road signs page or the rules of the road page — and then take this again.
