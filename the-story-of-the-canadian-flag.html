🚩 The Story of the Canadian Flag

The history of the Canadian flag — when the red and white maple leaf flag was created, what it means, and what came before it.

The red and white Canadian flag with its single maple leaf is known all around the world. But it is younger than many people think. Here is the story of how it came to be.

When did Canada get its flag?

Canada's maple leaf flag became official on February 15, 1965. Every year, that date is celebrated as National Flag of Canada Day.

What did Canada use before?

Before 1965, Canada mostly used flags based on the British flag, such as the Red Ensign. As Canada grew into its own independent country, many people felt it needed a flag that was clearly and only Canadian.

What does the flag mean?

The flag has two red bars on the sides and a white square in the middle with a red maple leaf. Red and white are Canada's official colours. The maple leaf stands for nature, unity, and the Canadian people. The design is simple, which is part of why it is so easy to recognize.

Flag facts

  • The maple leaf on the flag has 11 points.
  • Red and white were named Canada's official colours in 1921.
  • The flag is flown at schools, government buildings, and homes across the country.
  • You should treat the flag with respect and never let it touch the ground.
