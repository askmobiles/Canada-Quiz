🦫 Canada's Animals and Wildlife: A Guide for Families

Meet the amazing animals of Canada — beavers, moose, polar bears, loons, and more. A fun wildlife guide for kids and families.

Canada's forests, mountains, rivers, and Arctic are home to some amazing animals. Here are some of the most famous ones you can find across the country.

The beaver

The beaver is one of Canada's national symbols. These busy animals build dams and lodges out of sticks and mud. You can even find a beaver on the Canadian nickel (the five-cent coin).

The moose

The moose is the largest member of the deer family and can be found in forests across Canada. Males grow huge, flat antlers. Moose are strong swimmers and love wet, wooded areas.

The polar bear

Polar bears live in Canada's cold Arctic north. Canada is home to about half of all the world's polar bears. Under their thick white fur, polar bears actually have black skin to help them stay warm.

The common loon

The loon is a water bird known for its beautiful, haunting call. It appears on the Canadian one-dollar coin — which is why the coin is nicknamed the “loonie.”

More Canadian animals

  • Caribou — pictured on the Canadian quarter (25-cent coin)
  • Canada goose — famous for flying in a V shape
  • Grizzly bears and black bears
  • Beluga whales and orcas off the coasts
  • Snowy owls and bald eagles
