British Columbia Class 7L Mock Test

The same shape as the real BC knowledge test: 25 road sign questions, 25 rules of the road questions, a 45-minute timer, and a pass mark of 40 out of 50 counted across the whole test. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on to the next question. At the end you get your score, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

About this quiz

This page runs a full mock version of the British Columbia Class 7L knowledge test. Fifty questions appear, twenty-five on road signs and twenty-five on the rules of the road, with a forty-five-minute timer running across the whole thing. Questions are picked at random, so a second run is never the same test as the first.

The pass mark is forty out of fifty, counted across the whole test the way British Columbia counts it. There is no separate target to reach in each half, so a strong score on signs genuinely helps cover a weaker score on rules. That is a real difference from Ontario, where each half stands or falls alone.

This is unofficial practice, written in our own words from the free ICBC guide Learn to Drive Smart. We are not affiliated with ICBC or the Government of British Columbia. Test yourself here, then go back to the guide for whatever keeps catching you out.

What's inside

  • Fifty questions run in one sitting, split evenly between road signs and the rules of the road.
  • A forty-five-minute timer counts down, and when it reaches zero the test stops and your result appears.
  • Every answer is marked the moment you tap it, with a short explanation of why that choice is the right one.
  • The result screen shows PASS or TRY AGAIN against the forty-out-of-fifty mark used in British Columbia.
  • Each question you missed is listed at the end with the answer you chose, the correct answer and the reason.
  • The questions are drawn at random from a bank of one hundred and twenty, so the mix changes on every run.

Good to know

  • Forty-five minutes for fifty questions is a little under a minute each, which is comfortable once you have studied.
  • Because the score is added across the whole test, one shaky topic will not sink you on its own if the rest is solid.
  • Ten wrong answers is the most you can afford, so guessing quickly whenever a question looks hard is expensive.
  • When two answers both sound sensible, the safer and more patient one is usually the one the official guide supports.
  • Running the mock test several times brings up questions that the earlier runs never happened to select.

Common questions

Is this the official ICBC knowledge test?

No. It is unofficial practice written from the free Learn to Drive Smart guide. We have no connection to ICBC or the Government of British Columbia, and a pass here does not give you a learner licence.

What score do I need to pass this mock test?

Forty correct out of fifty, exactly like the real Class 7L test. The score is added across all fifty questions rather than half by half, so there is no separate target for signs and rules.

What happens when the forty-five minutes run out?

The test stops wherever you are and takes you to the result screen. Anything you never reached counts as wrong, so keep moving instead of stalling on one difficult question.

Can I see the questions I got wrong?

Yes. The result screen lists every missed question with the answer you picked, the correct answer and a short explanation. That list is the fastest guide to what you should study next.

Do I need to sign up before starting?

No account, no email and no download. The test starts in your browser as soon as the page opens, and you may come back and run it as often as you like.
