Quebec Class 5 Mock Test

Two sections of 12 questions with a 30-minute timer, and the real SAAQ pass mark of 75 percent applied to each section separately. SAAQ does not publish the number of questions on the real test, so we do not pretend to match it — what we match is the rule that actually decides whether you pass. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on to the next question. At the end you get your score, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

Keep studying

Missed a lot of sign questions? Go through the road signs study page, group by group. Struggling with the rules? The rules of the road page takes you chapter by chapter with no timer.

About this page

This is the timed Quebec practice exam. It draws a fresh set of questions each time you start, shows a short explanation after every answer, and lets you review what you got wrong at the end. Nothing is saved and no account is needed — close the page and your attempt disappears with it.

What you'll find here

  • A start screen explains the format before you begin.
  • Each question shows one road sign or one situation, with four answers and a running score.
  • A result screen tells you whether you passed and lets you review every question you missed.

Worth remembering

  • The SAAQ knowledge test is divided into three parts — the Highway Safety Code, road signs and signals, and driving behaviours — and you must reach 75% in each part separately, not just overall.
  • The SAAQ does not publish exactly how many questions its test contains, so our mock uses a fair and clearly stated approximation rather than pretending to a number nobody has confirmed.
  • Reading the explanation after a wrong answer is worth more than repeating the test. The reason sticks; the letter does not.

Common questions

Can I take this test more than once?

As many times as you like. Each attempt draws a different set from the bank, so repeating it tests your knowledge rather than your memory of the order.

Does the timer match the real test?

The clock is there to build the habit of answering at a steady pace. Practise with it running so the pressure feels ordinary on the day.
