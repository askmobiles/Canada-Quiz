🎡 Spin the Wheel

Spin it — then do whatever it lands on!

📖 How to play

Sit in a circle and take turns. Tap SPIN, wait for the wheel to stop, and read out the challenge that appears underneath. Do it, or pass to the next player. Pick a pack at the top — there are fourteen. Silly Challenges and Act It Out for laughs, Talk Time and Family & Memories for questions that get everyone talking, Move It! when the kids need to burn energy, Brain Teasers, Word Games and 30-Second Sprint for quick thinking, Draw & Create, Music & Sound, Animal Kingdom, Around the World and Canada Round for something different every spin, and ⭐ Advanced for the tricky ones. 🎲 Everything mixes all the packs together.

About this game

Put a phone or tablet in the middle of the table, tap spin, and the wheel picks something for you to do. It might be a silly dare, a question worth talking about, or a puzzle that makes the whole room go quiet for a moment.

It works at a birthday party, in a classroom on a rainy afternoon, or on a family evening when nobody can agree on what to play. There is no scoring and no winner, because the wheel simply keeps the turns moving.

How it works

  • Fourteen themed packs, plus an everything pack that mixes them all into one very long list.
  • Silly Challenges and Act It Out hand out dares, from talking like a robot to walking like a penguin.
  • Talk Time and Family and Memories ask questions that get a table talking instead of performing.
  • Move It gives children something physical to do, while 30-Second Sprint puts a short limit on every answer.
  • Canada Round, Around the World, Animal Kingdom, Music and Sound, Draw and Create, Word Games and Brain Teasers fill out the rest.
  • The wheel remembers what it has already given you, so a pack runs right through before anything repeats.

Tips for playing

  • Seat everyone in a circle and go clockwise. Deciding the order before the first spin stops the arguing about whose turn it is.
  • Allow a pass. One free skip per player keeps a shy child in the game instead of dreading every spin of the wheel.
  • For a large group, the everything pack is best, since the same kind of challenge is far less likely to come round twice in a row.
  • The advanced pack suits teenagers and adults, so try it once the youngest players have gone to bed for a different mood.
  • Talk Time works surprisingly well at a long dinner table, where a single question can hold a conversation for ten minutes.

Common questions

How many people do I need for this to work?

Two is enough and a dozen still works. With a big group, seat everyone where they can see the screen and let one person read each challenge out loud.

Can I choose a pack that avoids running around?

Yes. Talk Time, Family and Memories, Brain Teasers and Word Games all stay in a chair. Pick one at the top of the wheel and only those items will appear.

Will the same challenge come up twice in one evening?

Not until the pack is used up. The wheel works through everything in the chosen pack before starting that list over, which matters most in the smaller packs.

Do I need to print anything or buy cards first?

No. Everything lives on the page itself. One phone, tablet or laptop shared by the group is all the equipment this game will ever ask you for.
