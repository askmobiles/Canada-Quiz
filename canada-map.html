🍁 Map of Canada

Find all 13 provinces and territories, name them, and learn every capital.

📖 How to play

Pick a mode, then play 13 rounds. In Find It you tap the province or territory we name; in Name It and Capital Cities you choose the right answer out of four. Explore the Map lets you tap any place for its capital, its two-letter code and two facts.

    About this game

    The Map of Canada game puts all thirteen provinces and territories on one clickable map. You choose how you would like to be tested, by location, by name or by capital city, and then work through thirteen rounds, one for each place.

    It grew out of a simple problem. The names are easy enough to recite, yet far fewer people can point to Saskatchewan or tell Yukon apart from the Northwest Territories. Tapping the shapes fixes that faster than any written list.

    There is also a browsing mode with no scoring at all, for anyone who would rather explore than compete. Tap a province and a card opens showing its capital, its two-letter postal abbreviation and two facts about the place.

    How it works

    • Find It names a province or territory and asks you to tap its correct shape somewhere on the map.
    • Name It highlights a single shape while dimming the rest, then offers four names for you to choose between.
    • Capital Cities gives you the name of a place and four capitals, only one of which belongs to it.
    • Explore the Map turns scoring off, shows the two-letter labels, and opens a fact card for whatever you tap.
    • Each scored mode runs thirteen rounds and keeps your running score visible in the bar above the map.
    • The result screen lists exactly which places you missed and remembers your personal best for that mode.

    Tips for playing

    • Nunavut means our land in Inuktitut, and it was created in 1999 out of the eastern Northwest Territories.
    • New Brunswick is the only officially bilingual province, and the Bay of Fundy on its coast has the highest tides in the world.
    • Newfoundland and Labrador keeps its own time zone, half an hour ahead of Atlantic Time, a detail that surprises most visitors.
    • Saskatchewan takes its name from a Cree word meaning swift-flowing river, and it is one of only two provinces with straight-line borders.
    • Mount Logan in Yukon is the highest mountain in Canada, while the longest river, the Mackenzie, flows through the Northwest Territories.
    • If the map is new to you, start with Capital Cities: matching names to capitals gives you hooks before you go hunting for shapes.

    Common questions

    How many rounds does one scored game last?

    Thirteen, one for each province and territory, and the order is shuffled every time you start. A full game takes only a few minutes, so it fits into a break rather than an evening.

    Are my best map scores kept anywhere?

    Each mode keeps its own best score on the device you played on, so you can see whether you have beaten yourself. Nothing is sent away and there is no account sitting behind it.

    What happens if I tap the wrong province?

    Your choice flashes red for a moment, then the correct shape lights up green so you can see where it really was. The game moves on by itself a second later.

    Can I use the map without being tested at all?

    Explore the Map does exactly that. There is no score, no round counter and no pressure. Tap any province or territory and read its capital, its abbreviation and two facts about it.

    Does the map respond properly to a touch screen?

    It does. The provinces answer a finger tap the same way they answer a mouse click, and Prince Edward Island is given a slightly larger target so the smallest province stays reachable.
