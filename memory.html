🧠 Canada Memory Match

Flip two cards. Find the matching pairs. Good for all ages!

All the cards start face down. Tap one card to flip it, then tap another to find its match. If the two match, they stay open. If not, they flip back — try to remember where they were! Match all the pairs to win.
