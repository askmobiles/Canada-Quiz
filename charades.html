About this game

Charades Party turns one phone into the whole game. Split into two teams, hand the screen to the actor, and let them act out the secret word while their teammates shout guesses. No talking and no pointing at real objects.

It is built for four or more players in the same room, which makes it a natural fit for birthdays, family evenings and holiday gatherings. Scores are kept for you, so nobody has to hunt for a pen and paper.

Because you choose the categories before you begin, the same game works for a room of children and a room of adults. Pick cartoons and animals for one crowd, or films and superheroes for another.

How it works

  • Thirteen categories are available, among them Animals, Movies, Actions, Jobs, Objects, Sports, Video Games and TV Shows.
  • The remaining categories cover Cartoon Characters, Musical Instruments, Science, Countries and Superheroes, and any of them can be switched off.
  • Round length is chosen before the first turn, with forty-five seconds, sixty seconds, ninety seconds or two minutes on offer.
  • A handoff screen tells everyone to look away so that only the actor sees the word before the timer starts.
  • During a turn, a green button scores a point and moves on, while the Skip button swaps in a brand new word.
  • Team Red and Team Blue totals appear after every round, and a trophy screen names the winner when you finish.

Tips for playing

  • The peek button exists for the moment the actor forgets the word, and using it costs nothing, so never stand there acting out a blank.
  • Skipping early beats struggling for forty seconds on one impossible word, because a fresh word usually earns a point faster.
  • Big, whole-body movements read better across a noisy room than small hand gestures, especially for words in the Actions category.
  • Choosing fewer categories makes guessing easier, since teammates can limit their guesses to the few topics you left switched on.
  • A ninety-second round suits younger children, who need more time both to act out a word and to guess one.

Common questions

How many players does Charades Party need?

Four or more works best, split into two teams. With three people you can still take turns acting for each other, though the team scoreboard means less with such a small group.

Do we need more than one phone to play?

One device is enough. It passes from actor to actor, which is exactly why the game shows a handoff screen reminding everybody else not to peek at the word.

Can we stop in the middle of a game?

Yes. After any round you can end the game and see the final score, or carry on. Nothing is saved, so closing the tab simply finishes the party there.

Are the words suitable for children?

The categories cover everyday things such as animals, jobs, sports and cartoon characters. If one category feels too hard or too grown-up for your group, turn it off before you start.

How is the winning team decided?

Every correct guess gives the acting team one point. When you choose to end the game, the team with the higher total appears on the trophy screen as the winner.
