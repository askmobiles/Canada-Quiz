Manitoba Class 5L Mock Test

Two sections of 15 questions with a 30-minute timer, matching the time limit MPI publishes. MPI does not publish a question count or a pass mark, so ours is a clearly stated practice standard rather than a copy of the real one. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on to the next question. At the end you get your score, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

Keep studying

Missed a lot of sign questions? Go through the road signs study page, group by group. Struggling with the rules? The rules of the road page takes you chapter by chapter with no timer.

About this page

This is the timed Manitoba practice exam. It draws a fresh set of questions each time you start, shows a short explanation after every answer, and lets you review your mistakes at the end. Nothing is saved and no account is needed.

What you'll find here

  • A start screen explains the format before you begin.
  • Each question shows one sign or one situation, four answers, and your running score.
  • A result screen shows your score and lets you review every question you missed.

Worth remembering

  • Manitoba Public Insurance gives you thirty minutes for the knowledge test, and it covers three areas: traffic laws, safe driving practices and road sign recognition. The test is taken on a computer and it is not open book.
  • Sign recognition is the part most people underestimate. There are many signs and several of them look alike at a glance.
  • The real test is taken on a computer and it is not open book — phones and other devices are not allowed in the room.

Common questions

Can I retake this test?

As often as you like. Each attempt draws a different set, so repeating it tests what you know rather than what order you memorised.

Why is there a timer?

Because the real test has one. Practising against the clock makes the pace feel ordinary on the day instead of stressful.
