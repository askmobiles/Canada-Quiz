🗎 Printable quiz sheets

Pick a topic, choose how many questions, and print. The answer key prints on its own page so you can photocopy the questions on their own.

Free to use and to photocopy. No account, no email address, no watermark.

For classrooms, homes and community programs

Every sheet is free to print and free to photocopy — for a class, a tutoring session, a homeschool morning, a library programme, a settlement or language class, or a kitchen table on a rainy afternoon. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay, and no student ever needs an account, so no child's information is collected by this site at any point.

Pressing Print opens your browser's normal print window. Choosing “Save as PDF” as the destination gives you a file you can email or upload instead of printing on paper.

Each sheet is drawn fresh from the question banks, so pressing the button again gives a different set. That makes it easy to produce two versions of the same test, or a new one next term.

What is on the sheets

  • Canada — food, hockey, animals, provinces, symbols and everyday facts about the country
  • Citizenship practice — questions in the style of the real test, written from the official guide Discover Canada, 2012 edition
  • General knowledge — geography, science, animals, history, sport, maths and more, at three levels

How to use a sheet with a class

Make two sheets before you print anything. Press Make the sheet, print it, then press it again and print the second one. The questions are drawn fresh each time, so neighbours end up with different sheets, and you already have a spare version for anyone who was away.

The answer key always begins on a new sheet of paper. Page one is the questions and page two is the key, so you can photocopy page one thirty times and keep the single copy of the answers for yourself.

Every sheet already carries lines for a name, a date and a score across the top, and the site's address and the unofficial notice along the bottom — so a photocopy handed to a room of children still says where it came from.

As a rough guide, fifteen questions fill about twenty minutes if the group talks the answers through together, and twenty-five questions fill a period if they work on their own.

Saving a sheet as a PDF instead of printing

Every browser can turn the sheet into a PDF file, and there is nothing to install. Press Print as usual, then change where it is going:

  • Windows or a Chromebook — change Destination to Save as PDF.
  • Mac — open the PDF menu at the bottom left of the print window and choose Save as PDF.
  • iPhone or iPad — pinch outwards on the small preview to open it full screen, then use the share button to save it or send it.
  • Android — choose Save as PDF from the menu at the top of the print preview.

The file you get is exactly the sheet you saw, answer key still on its own page, and you can email it, put it in a shared folder or upload it to a class page.

Choosing a level

The Level control belongs to the general knowledge subjects only. Pick geography or science or maths and you can ask for easy, medium, hard, or a mix of all three. Easy suits the younger end of primary school, medium suits most of middle school, and hard will stretch a secondary class or the adults at a quiz night.

For the Canada and citizenship sheets the control switches itself off and reads All levels. Those two banks are not graded, because the real citizenship test is not graded either — it asks what it asks.

Common questions

May I photocopy these for my class?

Yes. Print one and photocopy it as many times as you need, for a class, a tutoring group, a library programme or a language class. There is no licence to read, no watermark on the page and no limit on copies.

Do I need an account or an email address?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay, and no student ever needs an account, so no child's information is collected by this site at any point.

Is the answer key printed on the same page as the questions?

No. The key always starts on a new sheet of paper, so the questions can be photocopied on their own without the answers going out with them.

Can I get a different set of questions?

Press Make the sheet again. Each sheet is drawn fresh from the question bank, so you can produce two versions of the same test for one room, or a new one next term.

Are the sheets available in French?

Yes. Switch the site to French and the sheets are generated in French, questions and answer key together, with the same printing and photocopying freedom.

Are the citizenship sheets the real test?

No. They are practice written from the official study guide, and this site has no connection to the Government of Canada. The real test is set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada and its questions are not published.

Please note: The citizenship sheets are practice only. This site is unofficial and is not affiliated with the Government of Canada. Always study the official Discover Canada guide for the real test.
