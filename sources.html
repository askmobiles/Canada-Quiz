Driving test practice

We cover all ten provinces and all three territories. For each one, the rules of the road, the sign meanings and the structure of the real test come from that jurisdiction's own driver handbook and licensing authority. Where a jurisdiction does not publish the number of questions on its knowledge test, its pass mark or its time limit, we say so on the page rather than inventing a number.

How we check things

  • Two sources for anything surprising. If a fact would make a reader raise an eyebrow, we do not publish it on one source alone.
  • Dates get the primary source. For anything constitutional we go to the text of the Act, not to a summary of it.
  • We show our disagreements. When Statistics Canada and a provincial government give different figures, we print both and explain the difference.
  • Every long article shows when it was last checked. A page about a changing situation is dated so you can judge for yourself whether it is still current.
  • We correct mistakes. If you find one, write to us and we will fix it and say that we did.
