About this quiz

Six light-hearted questions, one answer at the end: which Canadian province or territory best matches the way you like to live? It is a personality quiz rather than a knowledge test, so there is nothing to revise and nothing you can get wrong.

The questions ask about your perfect weekend, your ideal meal, the weather you enjoy, how your friends describe you and the home you dream about. Each choice quietly adds points to different provinces, and the highest total becomes your result.

It takes about a minute and it is made for sharing. Families often play it together around the table and compare answers, and it gives newcomers a friendly way to start picturing the different corners of the country.

What's inside

  • Six questions in total, each with four choices, and a progress bar showing how far along you have come.
  • One of seven results appears at the end: Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta, Nova Scotia, Manitoba or Nunavut.
  • Every result comes with a short description of the personality behind it, not simply the name of a place.
  • The choices cover weekends, food, weather, favourite activities, how friends see you and your dream home.
  • A Try Again button restarts the quiz instantly so you can see how one different answer changes the outcome.

Good to know

  • Canada is made up of ten provinces and three territories, and the results in this quiz are drawn from a mix of both.
  • Nunavut became Canada's newest territory in 1999, and it remains the largest and the least crowded part of the country.
  • British Columbia is the only province on the Pacific coast, and the Rocky Mountains run along its border with Alberta.
  • Nova Scotia is very nearly an island, joined to the mainland only by a narrow strip called the Isthmus of Chignecto.
  • Manitoba sits at the eastern edge of the Prairies, yet it also has a saltwater coastline on Hudson Bay in the north.

Common questions

Is there a right answer in this personality quiz?

No. Every choice simply adds points to different provinces, so no answer can be wrong. Two people can pick opposite things and both end up with a result that fits them.

How quickly can I finish the six questions?

About a minute. There are only six questions and one tap answers each of them, so it fits easily into a coffee break or a moment spent waiting in line.

Can I end up with a different province?

Yes. Press Try Again and change a couple of answers, and the totals shift. Many people run it twice on purpose, just to see which province comes second for them.

Does the quiz keep a copy of my answers?

No. Nothing is stored and no sign-up is asked for. Once you close the page your answers are gone, so take a screenshot of your result if you want to share it later.

Is this quiz suitable for children?

Yes. The questions are gentle, the wording is short, and every result describes its place warmly, which makes it a good one for a whole family to play together.
