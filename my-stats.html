Reading Google Analytics without getting lost

  1. Is anyone here now? Realtime. Open the site on your phone and you should appear within about thirty seconds.
  2. Which pages are working? Reports → Engagement → Pages and screens. This is the one that tells you what to build more of.
  3. Who finishes a quiz? Reports → Engagement → Events, and look for quiz_complete. Click it to see the score and pass rate. A page with many visits but few completions is a page that needs fixing.
  4. Where do people come from? Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition. "Organic Search" is the number that matters for the plan.

Events I set up for you: quiz_start · quiz_answer · quiz_complete (with score, percent, pass or fail) · engaged_15s · button_click. New quizzes are covered automatically.
