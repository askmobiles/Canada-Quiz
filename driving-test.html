How the practice works

Choose your province, then take the mock test. It uses the same number of questions, the same pass mark and the same time limit as the real test at the licensing office. After every question you see the correct answer and a short explanation, so you learn as you go. At the end you get a clear PASS or TRY AGAIN, plus a list of everything you missed.

There are also two study pages: one for road signs, grouped by type, and one for the rules of the road, chapter by chapter. Those have no timer, so you can take as long as you like.

Unofficial practice. Not affiliated with the Ministry of Transportation, ServiceOntario or DriveTest. Please study the free official MTO Driver’s Handbook to prepare for your real test.
