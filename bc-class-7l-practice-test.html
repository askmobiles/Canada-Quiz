What the real BC knowledge test looks like

The Class 7L knowledge test has 50 multiple-choice questions, and you need 40 correct answers to pass. Unlike some provinces, the score is counted across the whole test rather than half by half, so a strong result on signs does help make up for a weaker result on rules.

You take the test at ICBC, and you also need a vision screening and your identification. Our mock test gives you 50 questions with a 45-minute timer so the pace feels familiar before you sit the real one.

How to pass on your first try

Read the official guide once from start to finish, then use practice questions to find your weak spots. Most people lose marks on the signs, not the rules, because there are a lot of signs and many look similar. Study the signs in small groups rather than all at once, and pay attention to shape and colour: a red circle with a line through it always means you must not do something, a yellow diamond always warns you about what is coming, and an orange sign always means road work.

Take the mock test more than once. The questions are picked at random each time, so you will see a different test on every run. When you can pass three times in a row, you are ready to book the real one.

  1. There is no painted line and no marked crosswalk at this sign. Where must you bring your vehicle to a full stop?

    • About one car length past the corner of the cross street

    Without a stop line or crosswalk you stop where the cross street begins, then creep forward only if you need a better view.

  2. You meet this sign on a short ramp joining a busy highway. What does it ask of you?

    • Sound your horn and move over into the nearest lane

    Yielding means the other traffic keeps its right of way, so you time your entry to fit a real gap instead of forcing one.

    A posted limit is a maximum for good conditions; rain, snow or fog mean the safe speed is lower than the number.

  73. Where the limit is under 80 km/h, how slow must you go past a stopped emergency or maintenance vehicle?

    • 40 km/hCorrect answer
    • 30 km/h
    • The posted limit is fine
    • 60 km/h

    40 km/h. The rule covers police, fire, ambulance, tow trucks and highway maintenance vehicles displaying flashing lights.

  74. A pedestrian steps into a marked crosswalk ahead of you in BC. What must you do?

    • Yield and let them finish crossingCorrect answer
    • Pass behind them quickly
    • Sound your horn
    • Continue if your light is green

    Drivers must yield to pedestrians in a crosswalk, marked or unmarked. Never pass a vehicle that has stopped at a crosswalk — it may be stopped for someone you cannot see.

  75. What should you do before opening your door on a BC street with a bike lane?

    • Check your mirror and look over your shoulderCorrect answer
    • Open slowly, that is enough
    • Nothing, cyclists must avoid doors
    • Sound your horn first

    A cyclist hit by an opening door can be thrown into moving traffic. Some drivers use the far hand to open, which turns your body and forces you to look.

  76. You are driving a long descent in the BC mountains. How should you control your speed?

    • Shift to a lower gear and let the engine hold you backCorrect answer
    • Use the parking brake
    • Ride the brakes the whole way
    • Coast in neutral

    Constant braking heats the brakes until they fade and stop working. A lower gear does the work for you and keeps the brakes fresh for when you need them.

  77. Coastal BC gets heavy rain. When should you turn your headlights on?

    • Whenever you use your wipersCorrect answer
    • Only in fog
    • Only on the highway
    • Only after dark

    Lights make you visible to others, which matters more than how well you can see. Daytime running lights do not light your tail lights, so use full headlights in rain.

  78. What does a flashing green light at a BC intersection mean?

    • The light is pedestrian-controlled — be ready for it to changeCorrect answer
    • Turn left before oncoming traffic
    • Stop and proceed when clear
    • The light is broken

    This differs from Ontario. In BC a flashing green means the signal is controlled by a pedestrian push button, so it can change quickly when someone presses it.

  79. How long must you normally hold a BC Class 7L licence before the first road test?

    • 12 monthsCorrect answer
    • 3 months
    • 6 months
    • 24 months

    Twelve months in the learner stage. Passing the first road test moves you to Class 7N, where you display a green N.

  80. What does the green N sign on a BC vehicle tell other drivers?

    • The driver is in the novice stage of graduated licensingCorrect answer
    • The vehicle is new
    • The vehicle runs on electricity
    • The driver is a student

    N stands for novice. That driver has passed one road test but still has restrictions, including zero alcohol and a passenger limit.

  81. What is an immediate roadside prohibition in BC?

    • A licence suspension issued on the spot for impaired drivingCorrect answer
    • A warning letter
    • A vehicle inspection order
    • A parking ticket

    BC police can suspend your licence and impound your vehicle at the roadside. It applies at a blood alcohol level well below the criminal limit.

  82. You are tired on a long BC highway drive. What actually helps?

    • Stopping to rest or sleepCorrect answer
    • Opening the window
    • Turning up the radio
    • Drinking coffee and continuing

    Only rest fixes fatigue. Air and music mask the symptoms for a few minutes while your reaction time stays as slow as an impaired driver's.

  83. What must you do before driving away with snow on your BC vehicle?

    • Clear all windows, lights, mirrors and the roofCorrect answer
    • Clear the windshield only
    • Nothing, it blows off
    • Clear the front windows only

    Snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield the first time you brake, or flies into the car behind you at speed.

  84. How can you tell whether a tire has enough tread left?

    • Check the wear bars moulded into the groovesCorrect answer
    • Count the years since purchase
    • Look at the colour
    • Feel the temperature

    Every tire has raised wear bars between the grooves. When the tread wears down level with them, the tire is finished and must be replaced.

  85. You are in a collision in BC and someone is injured. What must you do?

    • Stop, call for help, and give your informationCorrect answer
    • Move the cars and continue
    • Call your insurer first
    • Exchange numbers and leave

    Stopping is a legal duty. Call 911 for any injury, then give your name, address, licence and insurance details to the others involved.

  86. Your vehicle skids on a wet BC road. What should you do with the accelerator?

    • Ease off it smoothlyCorrect answer
    • Press it harder
    • Nothing, keep it steady
    • Pump it

    Easing off returns grip to the tires. Braking or accelerating hard during a skid makes it worse — smooth inputs and eyes on your escape path are what recover the car.

  87. A BC transit bus signals to pull out from a stop. What must you do?

    • Yield to it where the sign requiresCorrect answer
    • Speed up to pass
    • Sound your horn
    • Stop completely

    On roads posted at 60 km/h or less, you must yield to a bus signalling to re-enter traffic. Slow down and let it in.

  88. What is the correct way to enter a BC highway from an on-ramp?

    • Match the speed of traffic, then merge into a gapCorrect answer
    • Use the shoulder until there is room
    • Stop at the end of the ramp
    • Enter slowly and let others adjust

    Use the acceleration lane to reach highway speed, check your blind spot, signal and merge. Stopping on a ramp is dangerous because traffic behind expects you to be moving.

  89. Which lane should you normally use on a BC multi-lane highway?

    • The right lane, keeping left for passingCorrect answer
    • The middle lane
    • Any lane you like
    • The left lane

    Keep right except to pass. Sitting in the left lane forces others to pass on your right, which is where their blind spot is worst.

  90. May a BC learner drive alone to a nearby store?

    • No, a qualified supervisor must always be in the front passenger seatCorrect answer
    • Yes, for short trips
    • Yes, during daylight
    • Yes, if under 5 km

    There is no exception for short or local trips. Driving without your supervisor is driving without a valid licence for that vehicle.

  91. How long must a British Columbia driver stay in the Novice (N) stage without a course?

    • 24 monthsCorrect answer
    • 6 months
    • 12 months
    • 36 months

    The Novice stage lasts 24 months, or 18 months if you complete an ICBC-approved driver training course.

  92. How many passengers may a British Columbia Novice (N) driver normally carry?

    • Only oneCorrect answer
    • As many as there are seat belts
    • Three
    • None at all

    A Novice driver may carry only one passenger, unless every passenger is immediate family, or a qualified supervisor is in the vehicle.

  93. Between which hours may a British Columbia Learner (L) drive?

    • 5 a.m. to midnightCorrect answer
    • Any time
    • 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.
    • Daylight hours only

    An L driver may drive only between 5 a.m. and midnight. The Novice stage has no curfew.

  94. What must a British Columbia L or N driver display on the vehicle?

    • The official L or N signCorrect answer
    • Nothing
    • A yellow triangle
    • A copy of the licence

    British Columbia requires the official L or N sign in the rear window, so other drivers know a new driver is at the wheel.

  95. When must winter tires or chains be used on designated British Columbia highways?

    • 1 October to 30 AprilCorrect answer
    • 1 November to 1 March
    • Only when it is snowing
    • They are never required

    On designated highways winter tires or chains are required from 1 October to 30 April. On some routes away from mountain passes the requirement ends 31 March. Watch for the signs.

  96. What is the minimum tread depth for a legal winter tire in British Columbia?

    • 3.5 mmCorrect answer
    • 1.6 mm
    • 6 mm
    • There is no minimum

    A winter tire must have at least 3.5 mm of tread and carry either the M+S marking or the three-peak mountain and snowflake symbol.

  97. Which phones may a British Columbia L or N driver use while driving?

    • None — all electronic devices are bannedCorrect answer
    • Hands-free only
    • Any phone on speaker
    • A phone in a dashboard mount

    L and N drivers may not use any electronic device, not even hands-free. A fully licensed driver may use hands-free, but a new driver may not.

  98. What is the penalty for using a hand-held phone in British Columbia?

    • $368 and 4 penalty pointsCorrect answer
    • $100 and no points
    • A warning for a first offence
    • $1,000 and a 30-day suspension

    Distracted driving in British Columbia costs $368 and 4 penalty points, and for a new driver those points come with far tighter limits.

  99. When may a child stop using a booster seat in British Columbia?

    • At age 9 or 145 cm, whichever comes firstCorrect answer
    • At age 6
    • At 18 kg
    • At age 12

    A child stays in a booster until they turn 9 or reach 145 cm, whichever comes first. Every child under 16 must be properly restrained.

  100. What is the speed limit in a British Columbia school zone during school days?

    • 30 km/h, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.Correct answer
    • 20 km/h
    • 40 km/h all day
    • 50 km/h

    School zones are 30 km/h from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on school days. Playground zones are also 30 km/h, but every day from dawn to dusk.

Worth remembering

  • British Columbia adds your score across the whole test, which is gentler than provinces that make you reach a mark in each half separately.
  • Most people lose marks on the signs rather than the rules, simply because there are many signs and several of them look alike.

  • Passing three practice runs in a row tells you more than passing once, because the questions are different every time.

Common questions

How many questions are on the real BC Class 7L knowledge test?

There are fifty multiple-choice questions and you need forty correct to pass, with forty-five minutes on the clock. Our mock test copies all three of those numbers so the pace feels familiar on the day.

Do I have to pass the signs and the rules separately?

No. British Columbia counts the whole test together, so forty correct out of fifty is a pass no matter which section those answers came from. That is different from Ontario, where each half is judged on its own.
