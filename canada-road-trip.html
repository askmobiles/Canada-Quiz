🚗 Road Trip Across Canada

Start on the Pacific and drive east. Every province you enter asks you its own questions, because every province writes its own rules. Answer wrong and the tank drops. Run it dry and the trip ends where you are.

Why the questions change at every border

Driving is not run from Ottawa. Each province and territory writes its own highway law, sets its own licence classes and its own test, and decides its own rules on the things learners ask about most: how long you must hold a learner's permit, who may sit beside you, what the alcohol limit is for a new driver, whether winter tires are required and when.

That is the thing this game is built to teach, and it is the reason it is a journey and not a quiz. Reading the sentence above changes nothing. Driving through Saskatchewan and then through Quebec, and being asked different things in each, is what makes it stick.

It matters most to people who move. A licence, a rule and a habit that were correct in one province are not automatically correct in the next, and the gap usually only shows up at a roadside stop.

How the trip works

  • You begin in British Columbia with five tanks of fuel and drive east, then north.
  • Each province asks two questions from its own bank before you reach the next border.
  • A wrong answer costs one tank, and the correct answer is shown with the reason before you drive on.
  • Run out and the trip ends where you are — the score is how far you got, not how many you ticked.
  • On the Ontario leg you get a drawn intersection instead of a written question, because those drawings come from the Ontario handbook and belong to Ontario only.

The route

Ten provinces from west to east, then the three territories. This is not one single highway and the game does not pretend it is — it is an order a person could actually drive, and a way of getting to all thirteen.

British Columbia · Alberta · Saskatchewan · Manitoba · Ontario · Quebec · New Brunswick · Nova Scotia · Prince Edward Island · Newfoundland and Labrador · Yukon · Northwest Territories · Nunavut

Common questions

Is this the real test?

No. It is practice, and this site is unofficial. The questions are written from each province's own published handbook, but the real test is set by that province and its questions are not published.

Do I need to finish in one go?

The trip is short on purpose — a few minutes end to end if you keep the tank full. Your furthest trip is remembered on your own device so you can try to beat it, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Which province should I practise properly?

The one where you will take the test. This game is a tour, not preparation for any single test — use the full practice test for your own province when you are getting ready.

Is it in French?

Yes, all of it, questions and explanations included. Every question in every provincial bank on this site was written in both languages from the start.
