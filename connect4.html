🔴🟡 Connect 4

Drop four in a row before your opponent does.

📖 How to play

Tap a column to drop your disc — it falls to the lowest empty slot. Get four of your colour in a row across, down or diagonally to win. Play a friend on the same phone, or take on the computer at easy, medium or hard.

 
All Games

About this game

Connect 4 is the disc-dropping duel most people know from the plastic board with the yellow frame. Here it lives in your browser: choose a column, watch the disc fall to the lowest free slot, and line up four before your opponent does.

Two people can share a single phone, passing it back and forth as red and yellow, or you can face the computer at three levels. Easy only spots wins and blocks one move away, while hard looks several moves ahead.

Wins are tallied under the board, which turns a single game into a series worth arguing about. New Game clears the grid for the next round, and Reset Scores wipes the running total when a champion needs humbling.

How it works

  • The grid is the standard seven columns across and six rows deep, and discs always stack upward from the bottom.
  • A line of four wins, counted horizontally, vertically or along either diagonal direction.
  • Four mode buttons sit above the board: two players on one device, plus the computer on easy, medium or hard.
  • Arrow buttons along the top let you aim a column precisely on a small screen before releasing a disc.
  • The winning four discs pulse and gain a white ring, and score pills below the board remember each side's wins.

Tips for playing

  • Opening in the middle column is the strongest first move, because more possible winning lines pass through the centre than any other file.
  • A double threat, where two different squares each complete a four, cannot be blocked in one turn. Building one is how games are won.
  • Diagonals are the lines people miss most often, so scan the board corner to corner before committing to a column.
  • Every disc you drop hands your opponent the square directly above it, so think about what you give away as well as what you take.
  • Connect 4 has been solved by computer analysis: with flawless play from the first move onward, the player who starts wins.

Common questions

Can two of us really play on the same phone?

Yes, that is what the two-player mode is for. One person plays red, the other yellow, and you pass the device across after each drop. No second account is needed.

What is the difference between the three computer levels?

Easy reacts only to this turn, taking an obvious win or blocking an obvious loss. Medium plans a few moves ahead. Hard searches much deeper and rarely gives away a free line.

Are my wins kept when I close the page?

The running score is stored in your own browser, so it is usually still there on your return. It disappears if you clear your data or press Reset Scores.

Does the board fit properly on a small screen?

It resizes itself to the space available, and the buttons above each column stay large enough for a thumb. On a narrow phone the mode labels shrink so all four still fit.
