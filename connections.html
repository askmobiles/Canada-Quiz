🔗 Word Groups

Sort the 16 words into 4 secret groups of 4!

There are 16 words that belong to 4 hidden groups of 4 (for example, "Canadian animals" or "colours"). Tap 4 words you think go together, then press Submit. If you're right, the group locks in with its colour. If you're wrong, you lose a life 💛 — you get 4 tries. Find all 4 groups to win!
