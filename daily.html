About this quiz

The Daily Challenge is a small ritual rather than a long sitting. It holds two tasks that change once every day: a five-question quiz and a single scrambled word. Everyone who opens the page on the same date receives exactly the same pair.

Because it is identical for everybody, it travels well between people. You can message a friend and ask how they did on question three, or compare scores at the dinner table, knowing you both faced the very same five.

Three counters at the top quietly track your habit: current day streak, best streak ever, and total days played. Finish the quiz or solve the word to keep the streak alive; skip a day and it begins again at one.

What's inside

  • The Daily Quiz is five multiple-choice questions answered one at a time, ending with a score out of five.
  • Questions come from a wide pool covering Canada, science, geography, animals, numbers and everyday facts.
  • The Daily Word gives you one scrambled word plus a written hint about its meaning, and a box for your guess.
  • Check tests what you typed, and Give up shows today's word so nothing keeps you stuck all afternoon.
  • A More daily fun panel links out to Sudoku, Word Master, Word Groups and Word Hive for a longer session.

Sample questions

  1. What is the capital city of Canada?

    Answer: Ottawa

  2. Which Canadian city is home to the CN Tower?

    Answer: Toronto

  3. How many players from one team are on the ice in hockey?

    Answer: 6

  4. Which of the Great Lakes has the largest surface area?

    Answer: Lake Superior

  5. Which great river flows north through Egypt?

    Answer: The Nile

Good to know

  • The day's quiz and word are chosen from the calendar date itself, which is why a player in Halifax and one in Victoria see the same puzzle.
  • Streak counters live in your browser alone. Clearing your browsing data or moving to another device starts the count again from zero.
  • Either task counts. Finishing the five questions marks the day as played, and so does solving the scrambled word correctly.
  • Scrambled words are easier if you hunt for a likely pair of letters first, such as CH or ST, then build outward from it.
  • The hint under the Daily Word describes what the word means, not how it is spelled, so read it before rearranging letters.

Common questions

When exactly does the challenge change over?

It follows the date on your own device, so a new quiz and a new word appear at midnight where you are. Come back the next morning and both sections will be waiting fresh.

What happens to my streak if I miss a day?

The current streak returns to one next time you play, but your best streak stays on record and your total days played keeps climbing. Nothing else is lost by taking a break.

How long do both parts take together?

Usually three to five minutes. The quiz is only five questions with no timer, and the scrambled word is a single puzzle, so the whole thing fits into a coffee break.

Are the questions suitable for the whole family?

Yes. They cover everyday general knowledge rather than specialist subjects, and the wording stays simple. Children often beat adults on the animal and space questions, which is half the fun.
