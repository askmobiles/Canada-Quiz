🗿 Inukshuk Builder

Stack the stones and build the tallest Inukshuk you can!

A stone slides back and forth at the top. Tap the picture, click, or press the Space bar to drop it onto the stack. The part that lines up stays — the part hanging over is chipped away, so each stone can get smaller. It speeds up as you climb. Miss the stack completely and the game ends.
