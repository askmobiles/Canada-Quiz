About this quiz

This is the fun trivia corner of the site: films and television, music, sport, food from around the world, video games, and books and cartoons. Questions run from cartoon characters a five year old knows to composers and world dishes that will test the grown-ups.

Play it on a car trip, at a family dinner or between two harder quizzes. Every answer comes with a small piece of background, so even the questions you miss leave you with a story worth repeating to someone later.

What's inside

  • Six categories: Movies and TV, Music, Sports, Food Around the World, Video Games, and Books and Cartoons.
  • Every category runs as a ten question round with four choices, scored as you go along.
  • The pool holds around one hundred and fifty questions, which keeps repeat rounds feeling fresh.
  • Correct answers turn green and wrong ones turn red instantly, with the right choice always highlighted.
  • Canadian names appear throughout, from Celine Dion and Drake to Mr. Dressup and his puppet friends.
  • When the round ends you can jump into another category directly from the results screen.

About three to five minutes. Ten questions, one tap each, and a single line of explanation. Most families play two or three categories back to back before they stop.

No. The mix is worldwide, with films, songs, athletes and dishes from many countries, plus a handful of Canadian names woven in because this is a Canadian site.

Books and Cartoons or Movies and TV usually feel friendliest, because most players already know the characters. Music and Food Around the World reach further and suit older players.

Yes. Play one round each in the same category and compare the two scores out loud. Nothing is stored between rounds, so both players begin from zero.
