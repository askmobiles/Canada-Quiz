🇨🇦 How to Prepare for the Canadian Citizenship Test (2026 Guide)

A simple, step-by-step guide to passing the Canadian citizenship test — what to study, how the test works, and free ways to practise.

Becoming a Canadian citizen is an exciting goal, and the citizenship test is one of the last steps on the way. The good news is that with steady study and a little practice, most people pass on their first try. This guide explains how the test works and the easiest way to get ready.

How the citizenship test works

The citizenship test has 20 questions, and you need to answer 15 correctly (75%) to pass. The questions are usually multiple choice, and you have 45 minutes to finish. Everything on the test comes from the official government study guide called Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship.

The questions cover Canada's history, geography, government, rights and responsibilities, and national symbols. You do not need to memorize the whole guide word for word — you need to understand the main ideas and remember key facts.

Step 1: Read the official Discover Canada guide

The Discover Canada guide is free from the Government of Canada website. It is the single most important thing to study, because every real question is based on it. Read it once all the way through to get the big picture, then go back chapter by chapter.

Step 2: Study one chapter at a time

Trying to learn everything at once is stressful. Instead, focus on one chapter at a time — for example, Canada's history one day, and how the government works another day. The main chapters include:

  • Rights and responsibilities of citizenship
  • Who we are and Canada's history
  • Modern Canada and how Canadians govern themselves
  • Federal elections and the justice system
  • Canadian symbols, the economy, and Canada's regions

Step 3: Practise with sample questions

Reading is not enough — practising real-style questions helps you remember facts and get used to the test format. Free practice tests let you see which topics you know well and which ones need more study. Every time you get a question wrong, go back to the guide and read that part again.

Step 4: Repeat until you pass easily

Do a few short practice sessions each day rather than one long session. Keep practising until you can score well above 15 out of 20 every time. When you can do that comfortably, you are ready.

Quick tips for test day

  • Get a good night's sleep and arrive early.
  • Read each question carefully before choosing.
  • Answer the easy questions first, then come back to harder ones.
  • Bring the identification and documents you are asked to bring.
  • Stay calm — you have plenty of time for 20 questions.
