🐝 Word Hive

Make words using these 7 letters!

📖 How to play

Make as many words as you can using the 7 letters. Every word must be at least 4 letters long and must use the middle (yellow) letter. You can use a letter more than once. Tap the letters or use your keyboard, then press Enter. Longer words score more points, and using all 7 letters is a "pangram" for a big bonus! Climb from Beginner to Genius.

About this game

Seven letters sit in a honeycomb, one of them in the middle. Your job is to build as many words as you can from those letters, and every word must contain the middle one. A letter may be used more than once in the same word.

It rewards patience rather than speed. There is no clock, so you can leave the page open, come back later, and add another word when one finally comes to you. Word lovers, crossword solvers and anyone building English vocabulary will find plenty here.

How it works

  • Words must be at least four letters long and must include the yellow letter in the centre of the hive.
  • Tap the letters or type them, then press Enter, while delete removes a letter and shuffle rearranges the outer six.
  • A four-letter word scores one point, and any longer word scores one point for every letter it contains.
  • Using all seven letters in a single word makes a pangram and adds seven bonus points on top.
  • Your rank climbs through Beginner, Good Start, Nice, Solid, Great and Amazing on the way to Genius.
  • Found words appear as chips below the hive, and a new puzzle deals a different set of seven letters.

Tips for playing

  • Look for common endings first. Adding -ing, -ed or -er to a short word you already found often produces another word on the list.
  • Every puzzle here has at least one pangram, so when your score feels stuck, try hard to use all seven letters at once.
  • Repeated letters are allowed, which opens up words that look impossible at first glance, such as doubling a letter in the middle.
  • The shuffle button changes nothing about the puzzle, yet seeing the same letters in a new order genuinely helps the eye spot fresh words.
  • Reaching Genius does not mean you found everything, because that rank arrives well before the full word list is complete.

Common questions

Why was my word rejected when it is a real word?

Each puzzle checks against a fixed list of accepted words. Some rare, technical or very new words simply are not on it, and that is no judgement on your vocabulary.

Is there a time limit on a puzzle?

None whatsoever. Take an hour if you want. The letters stay exactly as they are until you ask for a new puzzle, so you can think, walk away and come back.

How many different letter sets are there?

The page holds ten letter sets and picks one at random each time you start again, so you may well meet a familiar hive before you have seen them all.

Can children play this one?

Older children who read comfortably enjoy it, especially with an adult sitting beside them. Younger ones usually do better with a word scramble, where a hint points at one answer.
