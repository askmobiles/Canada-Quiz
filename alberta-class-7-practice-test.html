What the real Alberta knowledge test looks like

The Class 7 knowledge test has 30 multiple-choice questions, and you need 25 correct answers to pass. Unlike some provinces, the score is counted across the whole test rather than half by half, so a strong result on signs does help make up for a weaker result on rules.

You take the test at an Alberta registry agent, and you also need a vision screening and your identification. Our mock test gives you 30 questions with a 30-minute timer so the pace feels familiar before you sit the real one.

How to pass on your first try

Read the official guide once from start to finish, then use practice questions to find your weak spots. Most people lose marks on the signs, not the rules, because there are a lot of signs and many look similar. Study the signs in small groups rather than all at once, and pay attention to shape and colour: a red circle with a line through it always means you must not do something, a yellow diamond always warns you about what is coming, and an orange sign always means road work.

Take the mock test more than once. The questions are picked at random each time, so you will see a different test on every run. When you can pass three times in a row, you are ready to book the real one.

Road Signs

6 of 80

  1. You reach this sign at a country intersection where tall crops block your view to the left. What is the correct way to handle it?

    • Stop first, then edge forward until you can see, then go when clearCorrect answer
    • Sound your horn and roll slowly through the intersection without stopping
    • Stop only if you can see a vehicle coming toward you
    • Speed up so you spend less time inside the intersection

    The full stop comes first, then a slow creep to a spot where you can actually see the whole road.

  2. The road splits around a raised island and this sign is on the island. Which path is correct?

    • Pass to the right of the islandCorrect answer
    • Either side, whichever has less traffic
    • Pass to the left of the island
    • Stop before the island and let oncoming traffic clear

    Islands split traffic, and taking the wrong side puts you into vehicles coming the other way.

  3. You and an oncoming vehicle reach the bridge this sign warns of at the same moment. What is the safe move?

    • Slow down and be ready to let the other vehicle come through firstCorrect answer
    • Speed up so that you are onto the bridge well before the other driver
    • Move onto the shoulder and cross beside them
    • Sound your horn and hold your lane position

    A narrow bridge leaves no room for mistakes, so the safest driver is the one willing to wait.

  4. You are driving a rented moving truck and this sign shows a height lower than your load. What must you do?

    • Turn around and take a route with more roomCorrect answer
    • Let some air out of the tires and drive through slowly
    • Drive through in the centre, where the arch is highest
    • Follow another truck through at the same speed

    The number on the sign is the real limit, and drivers who guess end up wedged under the bridge.

  5. You and a vehicle on your right reach this flashing light at the same moment. Who goes first?

    • The vehicle on the right, after both of you have stoppedCorrect answer
    • You, because you are on the through road
    • Whichever driver moves forward first
    • The larger of the two vehicles

    A flashing red works exactly like a stop sign, and the usual right of way rules follow the stop.

  6. What is ahead when you see this sign?

    • Several curves in a rowCorrect answer
    • A roundabout
    • A dead end
    • One gentle bend

    Set your speed before the first curve. Braking in the middle of a bend unsettles the car when you need grip most.

Rules of the Road

6 of 100

  1. What is the youngest age you can get a Class 7 learner's licence in Alberta?

    • 14Correct answer
    • 12
    • 16
    • 18

    In Alberta you can apply for a Class 7 learner's licence at 14, and you need a parent's permission until you are 18.

  2. There is no speed limit sign on a street inside an Alberta city or town. What is the maximum speed?

    • 50 km/hCorrect answer
    • 40 km/h
    • 60 km/h
    • 80 km/h

    In Alberta the default limit inside an urban area is 50 km/h unless a sign shows something different.

  3. A highway maintenance truck is working ahead of you with its lights flashing, and there are two lanes in your direction. Besides slowing down, what does Alberta ask of you?

    • Move into the far lane if you can do it safelyCorrect answer
    • Turn on your four-way flashers
    • Sound your horn once as a warning
    • Come to a complete stop before going past

    In Alberta you must give roadside workers an empty lane when there is one, and slow right down when moving over is not safe.

  4. You are getting your vehicle ready for an Alberta winter. Which check matters most for seeing the road?

    • The wipers, the washer fluid and the defrosterCorrect answer
    • The paint and the wheel covers
    • The radio antenna and the speakers
    • The trunk lock and the spare key

    Slush freezes onto the glass in seconds, so wipers, cold-rated washer fluid and a working defroster are winter basics. ❄️

  5. Why is a chinook a hazard for Alberta drivers?

    • Melting then re-freezing leaves ice on the road, often overnightCorrect answer
    • It causes flooding
    • It has no effect on driving
    • It brings heavy fog only

    A warm chinook melts snow during the day; when the temperature drops again the meltwater freezes into a sheet of ice.

  6. Which document proves your Alberta vehicle is insured?

    • The pink insurance card issued by your insurerCorrect answer
    • The driver's licence
    • The registration
    • The bill of sale

    Carry the insurance card, the registration certificate and your licence whenever you drive. An officer may ask for all three.

Common questions

How many questions are on the real Alberta Class 7 knowledge test?

There are thirty multiple-choice questions and you need twenty-five correct to pass. The score is added up over the whole test, so a mistake on a sign question and a mistake on a rules question count exactly the same.

Is this website connected to the Government of Alberta?

No. We are not affiliated with the Government of Alberta or with any registry agent, and nothing you do here counts toward a licence. Everything is written in our own words as practice, and the free Alberta Basic Licence Driver's Guide remains the book to study.

Do I have to sign up or pay to use these Alberta pages?

Neither. There is no account, no email address and no download of any kind. The pages run in your browser, so you can practise on a phone while you wait somewhere and pick up again later.

Where do I take the real knowledge test in Alberta?

You take it at a registry agent office. The same visit includes a vision screening, and you must bring identification with you, so it helps to check what documents you need before you go.

Which of the three Alberta pages should I open first?

Start with the two study pages while the material is new, because neither of them is timed. Once the signs and the chapters feel comfortable, move to the mock test and see whether you clear twenty-five out of thirty.
