📋 Top Answers

Two teams guess the most popular answers. Game-show fun!

Two teams take turns. Read the question out loud. The team says an answer — if it's on the board, tap that row to reveal it and the points go to that team! If they guess wrong or run out of ideas, it's the other team's turn. When all answers are found, press Next Question. The team with the most points at the end wins. One person can be the host who taps to reveal.
