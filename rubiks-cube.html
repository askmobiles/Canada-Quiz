🧊 Rubik's Cube

Scramble it, twist the faces, and solve the 3D cube!

Press Scramble to mix up the cube. Then use the letter buttons to turn each face: U (up), D (down), L (left), R (right), F (front), B (back). Turn on Reverse ↺ to turn a face the other way. Drag the cube (or use the arrow buttons) to spin your view and see the other sides. Make every face a single solid colour to win! Tip: the middle square of each face never moves — that's the colour that side should become.
