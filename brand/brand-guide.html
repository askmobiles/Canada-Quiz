🍁 Canada Quiz — Brand Guide

Logo, colours and type for canada-quiz.com · July 2026

The logo

The mark is a friendly, rounded maple leaf in a soft red badge, with a small yellow question mark. The leaf says Canada, the question mark says quiz, and the rounded corners and warm colours say this is for families, not for bureaucrats.

Main version — use this one almost always

On a red or dark background

One colour — for printing, stamps, faxes, black-and-white photocopies

Stacked — when the space is narrow (posters, T-shirts, a square advert)

Icon and favicon — small spaces, app icon, browser tab

The favicon drops the question mark on purpose — below about 24 pixels it turns into a smudge. The plain leaf still reads clearly at 16 pixels.

Social sharing card

This is the picture that appears when someone shares the site on Facebook, WhatsApp or X.

Clear space and minimum size

Leave empty space around the logo equal to the height of the leaf badge. Nothing else goes inside that space — no text, no borders, no other pictures.

Never print the horizontal logo smaller than 120 pixels wide on screen or 32 mm on paper. Below that, use the icon on its own instead.

Maple Red is the main colour. Cream is the page background — not white; the warm cream is what makes the site feel gentle instead of clinical. The four accent colours are only for cards and buttons, never for the logo.

Type

There is no font to buy or install. Using the reader's own system font makes every page load instantly and look native on their phone — which is part of why the site is fast.

Voice

✅ Sounds like us
  • "Test your knowledge — 20 questions, no signup."
  • "Nice try! Here's why the answer is Winnipeg."
  • "Free forever. No account needed."
❌ Doesn't sound like us
  • "Unlock premium access to our proprietary question engine."
  • "WRONG."
  • "Sign up now to continue."

Warm, plain, encouraging. Short sentences. We are talking to a nervous newcomer studying for a real exam, a parent on the sofa with two kids, and a teacher with fifteen minutes to fill. All three should feel welcome and none should feel talked down to.

Rules

✅ Do
  • Use the SVG files wherever possible — they stay sharp at any size
  • Put the white logo on red, the dark logo on cream
  • Keep the leaf and the wordmark together at their built-in spacing
❌ Don't
  • Stretch, squash or rotate the logo
  • Recolour the leaf (no blue leaves, no rainbow leaves)
  • Add a drop shadow, outline or glow
  • Place the dark logo on a red or photographic background
  • Retype the wordmark in a different font
