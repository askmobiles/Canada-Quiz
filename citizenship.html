About this quiz

This page is a full practice run of the Canadian citizenship test: twenty multiple-choice questions, a thirty-minute clock, and a pass mark of fifteen correct. It is made for newcomers who want an honest picture of how ready they really are.

Each answer is marked the moment you tap it, and a short explanation appears underneath so you learn the reason instead of memorising a letter. At the end you see PASS or TRY AGAIN, your score, and every question you missed.

The questions follow the subjects covered in the official Discover Canada study guide. This is unofficial practice built by a small team, not by the Government of Canada, so keep reading the real guide beside it.

What's inside

  • Every attempt draws twenty questions at random from a bank of well over one hundred.
  • A thirty-minute countdown runs beside the question counter and closes the exam when it reaches zero.
  • Topics include Canadian history, government, rights and responsibilities, national symbols, federal elections and the regions.
  • Your browser remembers which questions you have seen, so a repeat attempt brings mostly new ones.
  • The mistake review shows your answer, the correct answer and the explanation together for each miss.

Good to know

  • The real test asks twenty questions and requires at least fifteen correct, a score of seventy-five percent — the same bar used here.
  • Discover Canada, the guide behind these questions, is published free by the Government of Canada and covers history, government and symbols.
  • Canada has ten provinces and three territories, and the guide devotes a chapter to regions far from where most applicants settle.
  • After the test comes the citizenship ceremony, where new citizens take the oath of citizenship together before a presiding official.

Common questions

Do I have to pay or register to take this practice test?

No. There is no fee, no account and no email box to fill in. Open the page, press the start button, and the twenty questions begin straight away.

Is a pass here the same as passing the real citizenship test?

No, and we would never claim it. This site has no connection to the Government of Canada and records no result. A good score simply means your studying is working.

What happens if the timer runs out before I finish?

The test stops on its own and shows the result you had earned, with a note that time ran out. You can start a fresh set of twenty right away.

Will I keep seeing the same twenty questions if I practise daily?

Not for a long while. The page prefers questions you have not been asked yet on each replay. Once the whole bank has been used, it resets and the cycle begins again.
