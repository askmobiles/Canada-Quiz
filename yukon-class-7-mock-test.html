Yukon Class 7 Mock Test

Two halves, signs and rules, with sixteen right needed out of twenty in each and thirty minutes on the clock. The 80 percent is Yukon's own pass mark, set in the Motor Vehicles Regulations for every class of licence. The question count, the timer and the split into two halves are our practice standard, because Yukon publishes a single combined test and neither a question count nor a time limit. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on. At the end you get your score for each half, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

When you are ready to book the real one

Take this three times on different days and look at where the misses land rather than at the total. If they cluster in the signs, go back to the sign page; if they cluster in the rules, go back to the rules page. Eighty percent leaves room for four wrong answers in twenty, and a weak chapter uses that room up quickly.

On the day, you sit the real test in person at a Motor Vehicles office and it costs twenty dollars. You are told whether you passed immediately, and if you did not you may write it again as early as the following day for another twenty dollars. Yukon publishes no waiting period longer than that, and its online service covers road-test booking rather than the written test.

Where to get the official information

Everything on this page comes from what Yukon publishes for free. The territory writes its own book, the Yukon Driver's Basic Handbook, and it is free to download from yukon.ca, so any site telling you that Yukon borrows another jurisdiction's handbook is simply wrong. There is an official French edition as well, the Guide de la route du Yukon. Whether the test itself can be sat in French is not published anywhere, even though the handbook and the licensing pages are bilingual, so we do not claim that it can. Read the handbook alongside this practice, not instead of it.

This is an unofficial free practice site. It is not affiliated with Yukon Motor Vehicles, with Highways and Public Works or with the Government of Yukon, and it cannot issue a licence. Always check the official pages for the current rules and fees before you book.

About this quiz

Common questions

Is this the real Yukon test?

No. It is free practice written for this site. The 80 percent pass mark is the territory's; the number of questions, the timer and the split into two halves are ours.

Why are the two halves scored separately?

Because it shows you where the weakness is. A total can hide a bad half, and going back to the right study page is the whole point. Yukon itself runs one combined test.

Can I take it more than once?

As often as you like. The questions are drawn at random each time, so the test is different on every run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for, and there is a full-screen mode for a phone or a tablet.

Where do I go if I fail a half?

Back to the study page for that half — the road signs page or the rules of the road page — and then take this again.
