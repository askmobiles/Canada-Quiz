🍁 Maple Says

Watch the pattern, then tap it back. One more colour every round — how far can you get?

📖 How to play

The board plays a pattern of colours, one at a time. When it finishes, tap the same colours back in the same order. Get it right and the pattern grows by one and plays again. Get it wrong and the round ends. Each colour has its own note, so after a while you start remembering the tune rather than the squares — that is the trick most people discover, and it is worth far more than staring hard. Turn the sound off if you need to, and the pattern still flashes exactly the same way.

About this game

Maple Says is a memory game. The board plays a pattern of colours one at a time, and you tap the same colours back in the same order. Get it right and the pattern grows by one and plays again.

Each colour has its own musical note, and that turns out to matter. Most people start by trying to memorise squares and then discover they are actually remembering a little tune. The tune is far easier to hold in your head, which is why the game is a genuine memory exercise rather than a test of concentration.

Three speeds. Easy flashes slowly, gives you three lives and lets you replay the pattern as often as you like. Medium is quicker with two lives. Hard is fast, gives one life and no replay, so a single slip ends the round. Sound can be switched off and the pattern still flashes exactly the same.

Tips for playing

  • Most adults manage somewhere between seven and nine before it falls apart, which lines up with how much short-term memory usually holds.
  • Saying the colours out loud, or humming the notes, reliably beats staring at the board. Sound and speech use a different part of memory than vision.

Common questions

Is there any sound if I am on silent?

No, and it does not matter. Every pattern is shown by the pads lighting up, so the game works perfectly with no sound at all.

Why do I get fewer lives on hard?

That is what makes it hard. Easy gives three lives and unlimited replays so younger players can enjoy it; hard gives one life and no replay.
