What the real New Brunswick written exam looks like

There are two written exams, not one. The Road Sign Recognition Exam covers the signs. The Rules of the Road Knowledge Exam covers everything else. Both are multiple choice, and both are available in English and in French.

Here is the honest part. New Brunswick does not publish how many questions either exam asks, and it does not publish the pass mark. Other practice sites state a number anyway. We looked for it on the government's own pages and it is not there, so this site does not state one either.

One timing is published: you get 30 minutes for the online version. Our mock test below uses that same 30 minutes, and asks twenty questions on signs and twenty on the rules with sixteen right needed on each. Those question numbers are ours, chosen as a sensible target, not the province's.

New Brunswick was the first province in Canada to let you sit the written exam online, at home, on a desktop or laptop with a camera. You can also sit it in person at a Service New Brunswick centre. Your results stay valid for six months.

Study the rules

Two levels, twenty-four months, and it counts experience not birthdays

You can start at 16. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must consent, and that consent has to be witnessed by somebody who is not a relative. Pass both written exams and a vision test and you hold a Class 7 Level 1 licence, with an L on the front of the card.

Level 1 lasts at least 12 months, or 8 if you graduate from a recognized licensed driving school. Then a road test moves you to Level 2, which lasts at least 12 months more. The whole programme takes a minimum of 24 months, and it is built around experience rather than age.

Then something unusual. New Brunswick states that no further road test is needed to move from Level 2 to a full Class 5 licence — but you do have to apply for it. Leaving it undone can bring sanctions, so put a reminder in your phone the day you reach Level 2.

The Level 1 rules that catch people out

A Level 1 driver cannot drive alone, and cannot carry any passenger except the supervising driver. Not a friend, not a brother, not one person in the back. Passengers start at Level 2, where you may carry up to three with only one in the front seat.

The person beside you must hold a Class 5 licence or better and have at least three years of driving experience, and they sit in the front passenger seat. There is also a curfew: no driving between midnight and 5 a.m.

Alcohol and drugs are zero tolerance, and the penalty is not a slap on the wrist: a one-year suspension. One thing this site will not tell you, because New Brunswick does not publish it, is any ban on driving on a divided highway. Unlike some provinces, no such restriction appears on the government's pages.

Where to get the official information

Everything on this page comes from what New Brunswick publishes for free. The New Brunswick Driver's Handbook is the book the real exams are written from. It comes in nine parts, and three of them do most of the work: the traffic signs part feeds the sign exam, and the rules of the road and safe driving parts feed the other one. It is free, in English and in French, on the province's own site.

One point where the official pages themselves disagree, so we will not pretend otherwise: the gnb.ca written-tests page says there is no waiting period to retake an exam, while Service New Brunswick describes waiting a day before a second attempt and a week before a third, with an in-person appointment required after three failures. The likeliest reading is that the waits apply to the online version. Check before you book.

This is an unofficial free practice site. It is not affiliated with Service New Brunswick or the Government of New Brunswick, and it cannot issue a licence. Always check the official pages for the current rules and fees before you book.

Road Sign Recognition Exam

  1. What must you do at this sign?

    • ✓Come to a complete stop, then go when it is safeCorrect answer
    • Slow down and keep rolling
    • Stop only if you see another vehicle
    • Sound your horn and continue

    A complete stop means the wheels stop turning. Stop at the line, or before the crosswalk if there is no line, then move off once the way is clear.

  2. What does this sign ask of you?

    • ✓Let traffic and pedestrians already there go firstCorrect answer
    • Stop every time
    • Speed up to merge
    • Turn right only

    Yield means give way. Slow down, be ready to stop, and go only when you will not force anybody else to brake or swerve.

  3. You see this sign at the mouth of a street. What does it mean?

    • ✓Do not enter, traffic comes the other wayCorrect answer
    • Entry allowed after 6 p.m.
    • Trucks only
    • Parking area ahead

    The street faces you the wrong way. Driving in puts you head-on against traffic, which is one of the most dangerous mistakes a new driver can make.

  4. What is this sign telling you?

    • ✓All traffic on this street moves in the direction of the arrowCorrect answer
    • The road bends in that direction
    • Turn here for parking
    • Two-way traffic starts here

    A one-way street. Every lane runs the same way, so watch for the sign before turning into any street you do not know.

  5. What does this sign prohibit?

    • ✓Turning left at this placeCorrect answer
    • Turning right at this place
    • Going straight through
    • Stopping at any time

    A red circle with a slash always means you must not do the thing shown. Here the thing shown is a left turn.

  6. What may you not do where this sign is posted?

    • ✓Turn around to go back the way you cameCorrect answer
    • Park on the left side
    • Change lanes
    • Turn right on a red light

    No U-turn. Drive on and turn around somewhere it is allowed and you can see well in both directions.

  7. What does this sign mean?

    • ✓You may not park here, but you may stop briefly to let someone outCorrect answer
    • You may not stop here for any reason
    • Parking is free here
    • Parking for delivery vehicles only

    No parking is not the same as no stopping. You may pause to let a passenger in or out, but you may not leave the vehicle parked.

  8. How is this sign stricter than a no parking sign?

    • ✓You may not stop at all, even to drop someone offCorrect answer
    • It only applies at night
    • It applies to trucks only
    • It allows fifteen minutes of parking

    No stopping means exactly that. Do not stop the vehicle here at all unless traffic or a police officer makes you.

  9. What are you being told here?

    • ✓Do not pass another vehicle along this stretch of roadCorrect answer
    • Do not change lanes in town
    • Passing lane ahead
    • Trucks may not use this road

    No passing. It is posted where you cannot see far enough ahead to overtake safely, such as a hill or a bend.

  10. What does this sign instruct?

    • ✓Pass to the right of the island or obstacle aheadCorrect answer
    • Right turn only
    • Merge right in 100 metres
    • Right lane closed

    Keep right. There is something in the road ahead — an island, a divider or a work area — and you go around it on the right.

  11. You approach this sign and a person steps onto the crossing. What do you do?

    • ✓Stop and let them cross the whole way before moving offCorrect answer
    • Drive around behind them
    • Sound your horn
    • Speed up to clear the crossing first

    The whole point of a marked crossing is that people on foot go first. Wait until they are safely across before you move.

  12. A school bus ahead has its red lights flashing. What must you do?

    • ✓Stop and wait until the lights go out and the bus moves offCorrect answer
    • Pass slowly on the left
    • Pass on the right
    • Sound your horn and go by

    Flashing red lights on a school bus mean children are getting on or off. Stop, and stay stopped until the lights stop flashing.

  13. What is this yellow sign warning you about?

    • ✓A school bus stop is aheadCorrect answer
    • A bus terminal is ahead
    • School buses are banned here
    • A bus lane begins

    A warning, not an order. Expect a school bus to be stopping ahead, and be ready for its red lights.

  14. You see this sign on a New Brunswick highway at dusk. What should you do?

    • ✓Slow down and scan both shoulders, because a moose may step outCorrect answer
    • Speed up to pass the area quickly
    • Turn on your high beams and keep your speed
    • Nothing, the sign is only for trucks

    New Brunswick publishes that more than 400 people here are involved in a moose-vehicle collision every year, and most happen between dusk and dawn. Slowing down is what buys you the time to stop.

  15. What does this sign mean?

    • ✓Deer often cross here, so watch the shouldersCorrect answer
    • A deer farm is ahead
    • Hunting is permitted here
    • A wildlife park entrance

    Where one animal crosses, another usually follows. If one runs across in front of you, slow down and expect a second.

  16. What is ahead when you see this sign?

    • ✓A curve to the right — slow down before you reach itCorrect answer
    • A right turn you must take
    • A right lane ending
    • A rest area on the right

    Slow down before the curve, not in it. Braking in the middle of a bend is how a car leaves the road.

  17. This sign warns of what?

    • ✓A series of curves, one after anotherCorrect answer
    • A single sharp turn
    • A winding river beside the road
    • A detour

    More than one bend ahead. Pick a speed you can hold through all of them rather than braking and accelerating between each.

  18. What does this sign warn about?

    • ✓The road may be slippery, especially when wetCorrect answer
    • Loose gravel ahead
    • A car wash ahead
    • Skidding is prohibited

    The surface can lose grip. Slow down, leave more room, and steer and brake gently.

  19. What is ahead?

    • ✓A bridge narrower than the road you are onCorrect answer
    • A bridge that is closed
    • A weight limit
    • A ferry crossing

    The road pinches in. Check for oncoming traffic before you commit, because there may not be room for two vehicles side by side.

  20. You see this sign. What is the safest thing to do?

    • ✓Shift to a lower gear and let the engine help slow youCorrect answer
    • Put the car in neutral to save fuel
    • Hold the brake down the whole way
    • Speed up so the hill does not slow you

    A lower gear does the braking for you. Riding the brake all the way down heats it until it fades, which is exactly when you need it.

  21. What does this sign tell you?

    • ✓Traffic ahead comes both ways — stay right of the centre lineCorrect answer
    • The road is closed ahead
    • A passing lane begins
    • Two lanes merge into one

    You are leaving a divided road for one where oncoming traffic shares the same surface. Keep right and do not drift.

  22. Traffic is joining from a ramp ahead. What is the courteous and safe response?

    • ✓Adjust your speed or move over to leave them roomCorrect answer
    • Hold your speed and your lane no matter what
    • Speed up so they cannot get in
    • Stop at the end of the merge lane

    Merging works when both drivers give a little. Adjusting early is easier and safer than braking hard at the last second.

  23. What is this sign preparing you for?

    • ✓A set of traffic lights you may not be able to see yetCorrect answer
    • A police checkpoint
    • A level crossing
    • A flashing beacon only

    It is posted where a hill, a bend or trees hide the lights. Cover the brake and be ready to stop.

  24. You see this sign. What should you do?

    • ✓Start slowing now, because a stop sign is coming that you cannot see yetCorrect answer
    • Stop right where the sign is
    • Stop only if traffic is coming
    • Nothing, it is for trucks

    It gives you the warning a hidden stop sign cannot. By the time you can see the stop sign, you should already be slow.

  25. What should this sign make you do?

    • ✓Watch for people walking and be ready to stopCorrect answer
    • Sound your horn to warn them
    • Move to the left lane and keep your speed
    • Nothing unless it is a school day

    People on foot have no bumper. Cover the brake, look well ahead, and expect somebody to step out.

  26. What does this sign mark?

    • ✓A school zone, where children may be crossingCorrect answer
    • A library
    • A bus depot
    • A playground with no road access

    Children do not judge speed and distance the way adults do. Slow right down and watch both sides of the road.

  27. What does this sign warn about?

    • ✓Children may run into the road from a play areaCorrect answer
    • A parking lot entrance
    • A sports stadium ahead
    • A picnic area

    A ball rolling into the road is usually followed by a child. Slow down and be ready.

  28. What is ahead when you see this round yellow sign?

    • ✓A railway crossingCorrect answer
    • A roundabout
    • A tunnel
    • A bridge under repair

    A train needs a long way to stop and cannot steer around you. Slow, look both ways, and listen with the window down if your view is poor.

  29. You are stopped at this sign and a train has just passed. What do you do?

    • ✓Check that a second train is not coming on another track before you crossCorrect answer
    • Cross immediately
    • Reverse away from the tracks
    • Wait exactly two minutes

    One train can hide another going the other way on a second track. Look again both ways before you move.

  30. What is ahead, and who has the right of way there?

    • ✓A roundabout — traffic already going round it goes firstCorrect answer
    • A roundabout — you go first
    • A detour — follow the arrows
    • A dead end — turn back

    Yield to the traffic already circulating, enter when there is a gap, and signal before you leave.

  31. An orange diamond sign appears. What does the colour tell you?

    • ✓Road work is aheadCorrect answer
    • A permanent hazard is ahead
    • A tourist attraction is ahead
    • A parking area is ahead

    Orange means temporary road work. Expect workers, equipment, changed lanes and a lower posted limit.

  32. This sign is posted before a work zone. What does it mean?

    • ✓A person will be directing traffic ahead — obey their signalsCorrect answer
    • The road is closed
    • A speed camera is ahead
    • A survey crew is measuring

    A flagperson's directions come before signs and lights. Slow right down and watch for the signal paddle.

  33. A traffic light is flashing red. What must you do?

    • ✓Treat it exactly like a stop signCorrect answer
    • Treat it like a yield sign
    • Drive through without slowing
    • Wait for it to turn green

    A flashing red is a stop sign made of light. Come to a full stop, then go when the way is clear.

  34. There is a solid yellow line on your side of the centre. What does it mean?

    • ✓Do not cross it to passCorrect answer
    • Passing is allowed if you are quick
    • It marks the edge of the road
    • It marks a bicycle lane

    A solid line on your side means no passing. It is painted where you cannot see far enough ahead to overtake safely.

Rules of the Road Knowledge Exam

  1. What is the beginner licence called in New Brunswick?

    • ✓Class 7 Level 1Correct answer
    • Class G1
    • Class 5 Learner
    • Class L1

    New Brunswick uses Class 7 Level 1, marked with an L on the front of the card. It is not called G1 — that is another province.

  2. What is the minimum age to get a Class 7 Level 1 licence?

    • ✓16Correct answer
    • 14
    • 18
    • 21

    Sixteen. If you are under 18 a parent or guardian must consent, and that consent has to be witnessed by somebody who is not a relative.

  3. How long must you normally hold Level 1 before moving to Level 2?

    • ✓12 months, or 8 months with a recognized driving schoolCorrect answer
    • 3 months, always
    • 24 months, always
    • There is no minimum

    Twelve months, reduced to eight if you graduate from a recognized licensed driving school. That four-month saving is the main reason people take the course.

  4. How long does the whole graduated programme take, at a minimum?

    • ✓24 monthsCorrect answer
    • 6 months
    • 12 months
    • 5 years

    At least 24 months in total: a minimum of 12 months at Level 1 and 12 more at Level 2. New Brunswick's programme is based on experience, not on your age.

  5. Who may sit beside a Level 1 driver?

    • ✓Someone with a Class 5 or better licence and at least 3 years of driving experienceCorrect answer
    • Anybody over 16 with a licence
    • Any adult, licensed or not
    • Another Level 1 driver

    Class 5 or better, and at least three years of experience. They must sit in the front passenger seat, where they can see what you see.

  6. Where must the supervising driver sit?

    • ✓In the front passenger seatCorrect answer
    • Anywhere in the vehicle
    • In the back seat
    • In a following vehicle

    The front passenger seat. From there they can see the road, the mirrors and the instruments, and reach the wheel if they truly must.

  7. May a Level 1 driver carry friends in the car?

    • ✓No — nobody may travel with them except the supervising driverCorrect answer
    • Yes, up to three
    • Yes, if they all wear seatbelts
    • Yes, during daylight only

    No. A Level 1 driver cannot drive alone and cannot carry any passenger other than the supervising driver. Level 2 is where passengers are allowed.

  8. Between which hours may a Level 1 driver not drive?

    • ✓Midnight and 5 a.m.Correct answer
    • 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
    • 9 p.m. and 7 a.m.
    • There is no time restriction

    Midnight to 5 a.m. Those are the hours when tiredness, poor light and impaired drivers all peak at once.

  9. How much alcohol may be in a Level 1 driver's blood?

    • ✓None at allCorrect answer
    • The same as any adult driver
    • A small amount with a meal
    • Any amount below the legal adult limit

    Zero. New Brunswick has zero tolerance for drugs and alcohol in the graduated programme, and breaking it brings a one-year suspension.

  10. How many passengers may a Level 2 driver carry?

    • ✓Up to three, with only one in the front seatCorrect answer
    • As many as there are seatbelts
    • None
    • Only family members

    Up to three passengers, and only one of them in the front seat. That is a Level 2 rule; at Level 1 nobody but the supervising driver may ride with you.

  11. Which two written exams must a new car driver pass in New Brunswick?

    • ✓A road sign recognition exam and a rules of the road examCorrect answer
    • One combined exam only
    • A rules exam and a first-aid exam
    • A sign exam and a mechanical exam

    Two separate exams: the Road Sign Recognition Exam and the Rules of the Road Non-Commercial Knowledge Exam. That is why this site keeps signs and rules as two study pages.

  12. How long are your written test results valid?

    • ✓Six monthsCorrect answer
    • One month
    • Two years
    • For life

    Six months. If you leave it longer than that before finishing the rest of the process, you have to sit the exams again.

  13. New Brunswick was the first province in Canada to do what with its written driver's test?

    • ✓Offer it online, taken at home with a webcamCorrect answer
    • Make it free
    • Remove it entirely
    • Offer it only by telephone

    New Brunswick was first in Canada to put the written test online, in 2020. It is taken on a desktop or laptop with a camera, and a proctoring system takes random snapshots.

  14. You want to sit the written test online. What do you need?

    • ✓A desktop or laptop with a working cameraCorrect answer
    • A smartphone
    • A tablet with no camera
    • Nothing but an internet connection

    A desktop or a laptop with a camera. During the test you cannot use a phone, wear earbuds, leave your seat or look anything up.

  15. In which languages can you take the New Brunswick written test?

    • ✓Both English and FrenchCorrect answer
    • English only
    • French only
    • English, with an interpreter for French

    Both. New Brunswick is Canada's only officially bilingual province, and testing is available in both official languages.

  16. After you pass the written exams, what happens before you get the licence?

    • ✓You wait at least four business days, then do a vision test at an SNB centreCorrect answer
    • The licence is mailed the same day
    • You take the road test immediately
    • Nothing else is required

    New Brunswick asks you to wait a minimum of four business days after passing, then go to a Service New Brunswick office for the vision screening. It catches a lot of people out.

  17. What eyesight standard must you meet?

    • ✓20/40 in your better eyeCorrect answer
    • 20/20 in both eyes
    • 20/100 in one eye
    • There is no vision requirement

    20/40 in the best eye. Glasses or contact lenses count, and if you need them to reach that standard, the licence says so.

  18. Going from Level 2 to a full Class 5 licence in New Brunswick requires what?

    • ✓Applying for it after the required time — the province states no further road test is neededCorrect answer
    • A second road test
    • A written test
    • An eye test only

    New Brunswick states that no further road test is required for the Class 5 upgrade, but you must apply. Not applying can bring sanctions, so do not simply let it sit.

  19. A school bus ahead is showing amber lights. What does that mean?

    • ✓It is about to stop — be ready for the red lightsCorrect answer
    • It has broken down
    • You must stop immediately
    • It is turning left

    Amber is the warning. Red is the order. When the reds come on, everyone stops.

  20. You are following a cyclist on a narrow road. What is the safe thing to do?

    • ✓Stay back until there is room to pass with plenty of spaceCorrect answer
    • Pass close so you clear the lane quickly
    • Sound your horn to move them over
    • Drive alongside them

    A bicycle can wobble, hit a pothole or swerve around a grate. Wait for room, then give them a wide berth.

  21. An emergency vehicle behind you has its lights and siren on. What do you do?

    • ✓Pull over to the right when it is safe and stop until it passesCorrect answer
    • Speed up to get out of the way
    • Stop where you are immediately
    • Move left and slow down

    Signal, move right, and stop clear of the lane. Stopping in the middle of the road or braking suddenly makes it harder, not easier.

  22. Why are large trucks harder to share the road with?

    • ✓They have big blind spots and need much longer to stopCorrect answer
    • They are always speeding
    • They cannot use their mirrors
    • They are not allowed to brake

    If you cannot see the driver's face in their mirror, they cannot see you. Never sit alongside a truck, and never cut in front of one.

  23. In New Brunswick, when do most moose-vehicle collisions happen?

    • ✓Between dusk and dawnCorrect answer
    • At midday
    • Only in winter
    • Only during a snowstorm

    The province publishes that most happen between dusk and dawn, and that the majority fall between May and October. A moose is dark and its eyes do not reflect your headlights the way a deer's do.

  24. Why is hitting a moose more dangerous than hitting a smaller animal?

    • ✓It can weigh up to 450 kg and its long legs put the body into the windshieldCorrect answer
    • It is more likely to run away
    • It always steps aside at the last moment
    • It weighs less than a deer

    New Brunswick publishes that a moose can weigh up to 450 kg. Because the legs are long, a car goes under the body and the body comes through the glass.

  25. An animal runs across the road in front of you. What is the safest response?

    • ✓Brake firmly in a straight line rather than swervingCorrect answer
    • Swerve hard around it
    • Accelerate past it
    • Close your eyes and brake

    Swerving is how a survivable collision becomes a rollover or a head-on. Brake hard, stay in your lane, and keep looking where you want to go.

  26. Fog has closed in. What should you use?

    • ✓Low beamsCorrect answer
    • High beams
    • Hazard lights while moving
    • No lights at all

    High beams bounce off fog and blind you. Low beams throw the light under it. Slow down and increase your following distance.

  27. What is the safest way to deal with a text message while driving?

    • ✓Do not read or send it until you have parkedCorrect answer
    • Read it at a red light
    • Reply using one hand
    • Dictate it while driving

    Anything that takes your eyes off the road takes them off it for longer than you think. A car covers a great deal of ground in the seconds you are looking down.

  28. You are getting drowsy on a long drive. What actually works?

    • ✓Stop somewhere safe and restCorrect answer
    • Open the window and turn up the radio
    • Drink coffee and keep going
    • Drive faster to arrive sooner

    Nothing but sleep fixes sleepiness. Cold air and loud music postpone it by a few minutes, which is not long enough to matter.

  29. What is the point of checking your mirrors every few seconds?

    • ✓So you already know what is around you before you need to actCorrect answer
    • To check your appearance
    • It is only needed before turning
    • To watch for police

    A driver who knows what is beside and behind them can react at once. A driver who has to look first has already lost the time they needed.

  30. Your windshield washer bottle is empty in winter. Why does it matter?

    • ✓Salt spray blinds the windshield and you cannot clear itCorrect answer
    • The engine will overheat
    • The wipers will not turn on
    • It does not matter

    One truck passing on a slushy road can coat the glass in seconds. With no washer fluid there is nothing you can do but pull over.

  31. Why check tire pressure regularly?

    • ✓Under-inflated tires grip and stop worse and wear out fasterCorrect answer
    • It makes the car quieter
    • It improves the radio reception
    • Only new drivers need to

    Tires are the only part of the car touching the road. A soft tire changes how the car steers and how far it takes to stop.

  32. You are in a collision with injuries. What is the first thing to do?

    • ✓Make sure the scene is safe and call for helpCorrect answer
    • Move all vehicles immediately
    • Take photographs first
    • Drive to the nearest garage

    Safety first, then help. Turn on your hazard lights, do not stand in traffic, and call emergency services before anything else.

  33. Your vehicle breaks down on a highway shoulder at night. What is safest?

    • ✓Stay belted inside with the hazard lights on and call for helpCorrect answer
    • Stand behind the car to warn traffic
    • Walk along the lane to the next exit
    • Sit on the guardrail facing away from traffic

    Standing near fast-moving traffic in the dark is far more dangerous than the breakdown. Stay inside, belted, with the hazards on.

About this page

New Brunswick sets two separate written exams for a new car driver: a Road Sign Recognition Exam and a Rules of the Road Knowledge Exam. Both are multiple choice and both are offered in English and French, in person at a Service New Brunswick centre or online at home. That is why this site keeps signs and rules as two separate study pages.

New Brunswick does not publish how many questions either exam asks, or what the pass mark is. We looked on gnb.ca and on Service New Brunswick's pages, in both languages, and neither number is there. So the numbers on our mock test are ours, and the pages say so. The one timing the province does publish, 30 minutes for the online exam, is the clock we use.

Common questions

How many questions are on the New Brunswick written exam?

New Brunswick does not publish a number, and neither does Service New Brunswick. There are two exams — one on road signs and one on the rules of the road — but no government page states how many questions either one asks. Any site that gives you a figure is guessing.

What is the pass mark?

It is not published either. Practice sites commonly say eighty percent, but that figure does not appear on any New Brunswick government page, so this site will not state it as fact. Study the handbook properly and the question stops mattering.

Can I take the exam online?

Yes. New Brunswick was the first province in Canada to offer it, on a desktop or laptop with a camera. You get 30 minutes, and you cannot use a phone, wear earbuds or leave your seat. You can also sit it in person at a Service New Brunswick centre.

Is the exam available in French?

Yes. New Brunswick is Canada's only officially bilingual province and testing is available in both official languages. The driver's handbook is published in both as well.

Who can supervise me while I drive on a Level 1 licence?

Somebody holding a Class 5 licence or better with at least three years of driving experience, sitting in the front passenger seat. At Level 1 you cannot drive alone and you cannot carry any other passenger.

How long before I get a full licence?

At least 24 months: 12 at Level 1, or 8 with a recognized driving school, then a road test, then 12 more at Level 2. New Brunswick states no further road test is needed for the Class 5 upgrade, but you must apply for it.
