🔗 Word Groups

Sort the 16 words into 4 secret groups of 4!

📖 How to play

There are 16 words that belong to 4 hidden groups of 4 (for example, "Canadian animals" or "colours"). Tap 4 words you think go together, then press Submit. If you're right, the group locks in with its colour. If you're wrong, you lose a life 💛 — you get 4 tries. Find all 4 groups to win!

About this game

Word Groups puts sixteen words in front of you and hides four themes among them. Choose the four words you believe belong together, press Submit, and watch the group lock into place with its own colour if you were right.

The difficulty comes from overlap. A word can look at home in two separate themes, and the puzzles are arranged so the obvious foursome is often not the intended one. Beginning with the group you are surest about is the safest habit.

You have four lives. Each wrong guess costs one, and when the last is gone the puzzle reveals the answers rather than leaving you wondering. Several themes are Canadian, so families here start with a small home advantage.

How it works

  • Sixteen tiles fill the grid and exactly four of them belong to each of the four hidden themes.
  • Tap up to four tiles to select them, tap a chosen tile again to release it, then press Submit.
  • When three of your four words are correct, the game tells you that you were one away.
  • A row of hearts shows your remaining lives, and the grid shakes briefly whenever a guess misses.
  • Themes range from Canadian animals, provinces, hockey words and coins to everyday sets like fruits and feelings.

Tips for playing

  • Always solve the group you are most certain of first. Every locked group removes four tiles and makes what remains far easier to read.
  • If one word could belong to two themes, set it aside until you have solved the theme it does not belong to.
  • Wide-sounding themes such as winter things are usually the trap, because half the grid could plausibly qualify for them.
  • Say your four words aloud with the theme name attached before submitting. If one sounds forced, it almost certainly is.
  • Reordering the tiles with Shuffle costs nothing and can dissolve a wrong pattern your eyes have stopped questioning.

Common questions

How many different puzzles can I get here?

The page holds several complete puzzles and picks one at random each time. Pressing New Puzzle deals another, so a family can play a few rounds without meeting the same words at once.

What happens when I lose my last life?

The game stops accepting guesses and shows the remaining groups with their theme names, so you finish knowing the answer. A new puzzle can be started immediately afterwards.

Is this a good puzzle for children?

The vocabulary is simple enough for most school-age readers and the themes are everyday ones. Younger players do better with an adult reading the tiles aloud and talking through the choices.

Can I change my mind after tapping a tile?

Yes. Nothing is committed until you press Submit. Tap any selected tile a second time to deselect it, and swap words around as often as you like beforehand.
