🎨 Draw It

Draw the secret word — your team guesses before time runs out!

📖 How to play

Pick a word pack, then press New word. Only the artist looks at the word — everyone else looks away for a second! Press Start drawing and draw on the white board with your finger or mouse. No letters, no numbers, no talking. If your team guesses in time, press the team's +1 button. Then pass the device to the next artist. Choose ⭐ Advanced for much harder words.

About this game

Draw It turns one phone, tablet or laptop into a shared drawing board. One player looks at a secret word, draws it with a finger or a mouse, and everyone else shouts guesses. It is the classic drawing-and-guessing party game, ready to play in seconds.

It fits a family evening, a birthday party or a rainy afternoon with cousins. Nobody needs paper, pencils or a printer. Younger children can stay with the easy pack, while adults who want a real challenge switch to the advanced words and draw an idea instead of an object.

How it works

  • Five word packs are built in: Easy, Canada, Movies and TV, Advanced, and Advanced Actions for acted-out ideas.
  • The Canada pack hides words such as beaver, toque, inukshuk, curling stone, canoe and northern lights.
  • A ninety second countdown sits above the board and turns red for the final ten seconds of the round.
  • The Red team and the Blue team each have their own score and a plus one button you press after a correct guess.
  • The drawing tools give you nine colours, a white eraser, a brush size slider, an undo button and a clear button.

Tips for playing

  • Sketch the biggest shape first. A rough outline that fills the whole board is easier to read than tiny careful details.
  • The artist must not write letters or numbers and must not speak, but arrows and circles are allowed and help a lot.
  • Advanced words like homesick, recycling or rush hour are ideas, not objects, so draw a small scene with people in it.
  • Thick lines and strong colours show up much better when everyone is crowded around one small phone screen.
  • Press plus one as soon as the guess is right, because that also stops the clock and settles the round.

Common questions

Do we need paper and pencils to play Draw It?

No. The whole game happens on the screen. One device is passed around the room, and each new word wipes the board clean, so a single phone is enough for a whole group.

How many players does Draw It need?

Three is enough, one artist and two guessers, but it is livelier with two full teams. The scoreboard is built for a red team and a blue team playing against each other.

Are the drawings saved or shared anywhere?

No drawing leaves the device. Pressing Clear or choosing a new word erases the board, and nothing is uploaded or stored, so children can scribble as freely as they like.

Can we make the rounds longer than ninety seconds?

The timer is fixed at ninety seconds, but Start drawing can be pressed again to restart it whenever a round is interrupted. Plenty of groups simply keep guessing after the buzzer.
