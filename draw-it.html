🎨 Draw It

Draw the secret word — your team guesses before time runs out!

📖 How to play

Pick a word pack, then press New word. Only the artist looks at the word — everyone else looks away for a second! Press Start drawing and draw on the white board with your finger or mouse. No letters, no numbers, no talking. If your team guesses in time, press the team's +1 button. Then pass the device to the next artist. Choose ⭐ Advanced for much harder words.
