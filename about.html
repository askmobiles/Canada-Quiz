🍁 About Us

Free quizzes and family games for everyone — no signup, no cost, no app to download.

Canada Quiz & Family Fun Games is an independent Canadian website, online since 2011. We make free quizzes, family and kids' party games, learner driving test practice, and unofficial practice for the Canadian citizenship test.

Our citizenship practice

Our practice questions are written based on the official Discover Canada study guide published by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada. Each question includes an explanation, because understanding the answer matters more than memorizing it. The practice test follows the real format — 20 questions, with 15 correct needed to pass — so the experience feels close to the real thing. We review our questions against official materials and update them when those materials change.

Important: This site is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of Canada. It is for study and practice only. Always study the official Discover Canada guide to prepare for your real test.

How we work

Our content is researched and written with the assistance of AI tools, then reviewed, edited, and fact-checked against official sources before it is published. A person reads every question before it goes live.

Who this is for

Families looking for something to play together. Kids who want a game of their own. Students and teachers who want a quick classroom activity. Newcomers preparing for the citizenship test. New drivers studying for a learner's licence. And anyone who likes learning something they didn't know before.

Found a mistake?

We'd rather hear about it than leave it wrong. If you spot an incorrect answer or an outdated question, tell us which quiz and which question, and we'll review it. Visit our Contact page or email info@canada-quiz.com.
