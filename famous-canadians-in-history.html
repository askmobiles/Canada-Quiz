⭐ Famous Canadians in History Everyone Should Know

Meet some of the most famous Canadians in history — inventors, leaders, athletes, and heroes who shaped the country.

Canada has produced many remarkable people who changed the country and the world. Learning about them is a fun way to understand Canadian history. Here are a few famous Canadians worth knowing.

Sir John A. Macdonald

Sir John A. Macdonald was Canada's first prime minister. He played a leading role in bringing the colonies together to form Canada in 1867 and in building a railway that connected the country from coast to coast.

Terry Fox

Terry Fox is one of Canada's greatest heroes. After losing a leg to cancer, he ran across much of Canada in 1980 on an artificial leg to raise money for cancer research. His Marathon of Hope inspired millions, and runs are still held in his name every year.

Sir Frederick Banting

Sir Frederick Banting was a Canadian doctor who, with his team, discovered insulin in the 1920s. This discovery saved the lives of millions of people with diabetes around the world.

Wayne Gretzky

Wayne Gretzky is often called the greatest hockey player of all time. Nicknamed “The Great One,” he set many records in the NHL and helped make hockey even more popular in Canada and beyond.

Viola Desmond

Viola Desmond was a Black Canadian businesswoman who stood up against unfair treatment in 1946, long before the civil rights movement became well known. Today her portrait appears on the Canadian ten-dollar bill.
