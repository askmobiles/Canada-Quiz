Northwest Territories Class 7 Mock Test

Two tests, road signage and the rules of the road, with sixteen right needed out of twenty in each and each one passing or failing on its own. That is the government's own published format and not a standard of ours. The thirty minutes on the clock is the published figure, although the government does not say whether it covers one test or both. Questions are picked at random, so every run is a new test.

📖 How to play

Tap an answer to lock it in. You will see straight away whether it was right, with a short explanation, then move on. At the end you get your score for each test, a PASS or TRY AGAIN, and a list of every question you missed.

When you are ready to book the real one

Take this three times on different days and look at which test the misses land in rather than at the total. A total is exactly what will mislead you here, because sixteen of twenty is required on each test separately and a strong half can carry a weak one right up to the day it counts.

When you are ready, you book with Driver and Vehicle Services, online or in person, and you sit the test at a driver and vehicle office. Booking online is not the same as writing online, and nothing the territory publishes says the knowledge test itself can be written at home. A vision screening is required before the licence is issued. If you fail, the official wording is only that a new test must be scheduled, and no waiting period is published.

Where to get the official information

Everything on this page comes from what the Northwest Territories publishes for free. The Basic Driver's Manual is the book the real test is written from, it is free to download from the territory's own site, and there is an official French edition, the Manuel du conducteur. The Northwest Territories has eleven official languages and publishes its driver manuals in English and in French, but which languages the examination itself is administered in is not published anywhere, so we do not claim it is offered in one language or another. Read the manual alongside this practice, not instead of it.

This is an unofficial free practice site. It is not affiliated with Driver and Vehicle Services, the Department of Infrastructure or the Government of the Northwest Territories, and it cannot issue a licence. Always check the official pages for the current rules and fees before you book.

About this quiz

This is the timed version, and it follows the format the Northwest Territories publishes. Two tests are drawn at random from the territory's bank, road signage and the rules of the road, and each one is scored on its own.

The numbers here are the government's own and not a standard of ours. Twenty questions in each test, sixteen right needed in each, and thirty minutes on the clock. The one honest gap is that the published wording gives thirty minutes to the test in the singular, without saying whether it covers one test or both.

Because the questions are picked at random each time, every run is a different test. Take it on three separate days before you decide you are ready.

What's inside

  • Twenty road signage questions and twenty rules questions, drawn at random from the sixty-question bank.
  • Sixteen correct answers needed in each test, which is the territory's own published pass mark.
  • A thirty-minute countdown, which is the published figure for the test.
  • A short explanation after every answer, right or wrong.
  • A pass or try again result for each test, scored separately, the way the real one is.
  • A list of every question you missed, so you know which page to go back to.

Good to know

  • Each test passes or fails on its own, so a strong half cannot rescue a weak one.
  • One examination fee covers both parts, and the fee schedule lists the Class 7 signs test as included.
  • We do not quote a fee amount, because the official booking page currently shows two conflicting fee tables under one heading.
  • Booking is done online or in person, but nothing official says the knowledge test itself can be written online.
  • If you fail, the official wording is only that a new test must be scheduled, and no waiting period is published.

Common questions

Is this the real Northwest Territories test?

No. It is free practice written for this site. The format is the real one, though: two tests of twenty questions with sixteen right needed in each.

Why are the two tests scored separately?

Because that is how the territory scores them. You may not miss more than four questions in each test, so a total out of forty tells you nothing useful.

Can I take it more than once?

As often as you like. The questions are drawn at random each time, so the test is different on every run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for, and there is a full-screen mode for a phone or a tablet.

Where do I go if I fail one of the two?

Back to the study page for that test — the road signs page or the rules of the road page — and then take this again.
